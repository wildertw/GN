2025 · Week 4

NFL COMP

Injuries, injuries, and more injuries. Malik Nabers knee. Joe Burrow’s toe. James Conner's Achilles. Trey Wilders's broken heart. I threw out a cool new stat showing which players have dominated your opponent in the weekly tale of the tape and not one you asshole even said cool. I would have gladly even taken the sunglasses emoji from just one of you. Not even a like on the picture. Fuck you guys.

1 - Ian - Chiefs - The Baro dynasty and the Chiefs dynasty have gone hand in hand. Sharing Super Bowl wins in 2020 and 2023. To the surprise of no one after stumbling out of the gate, Ian’s team has come on strong. Just like the Chiefs, the Baro motto has always been next up. Keenan Allen is just the latest player to be blessed with the fountain of youth that comes with being on team Baro. The similarities don’t stop there, losing Tyreek Hill will hurt but we all know they will find a way to be playing in late December without him

2 - Hud - Chargers - In 2010 San Diego Chargers famously missed the playoffs despite having the NFL's No. 1 ranked offense and No. 1 ranked defense. Last year, despite leading the league in Points Scored, Hud missed the playoffs. Last week, Hud saw his career long nine game winning streak come to halt. The schedule maker did Hud no favors as he plays one seed Ian. Luckily for both of them this will be their last regular season meeting of the year. Hud does not look like Phillip Rivers anymore. Stop saying that.

3 - Cody - Colts - The surprise of the NFL season thus far has to be Indianapolis Jones and the surprise of our league has to be Clod. The decision to not keep the current number one overall player in the offseason has not phased team Clod to this point. Having Potter, Ian, and Hud on the schedule for the closing stretch will make these next few weeks that much more critical for the weeks to come.

4 - Rex - Packers - Every year the Packers are in the playoffs, and it feels like you can say the same for Rex having made the playoffs ten out of the sixteen seasons. Will keeping Drake London over Packer Josh Jacobs come back to haunt him? The injury room in Rex’s locker room seems to keep growing. Should it bother him that he bid $27 on Drake Maye this week and no one else even put a bid on him? Somehow outbidding Wood for Rachaad White for $4 has to make Rex a little happy.

5 - Potter - Patriots - Part of the reason I haven’t ever done this comparison is this. I only put this here because I had to. If the Packers/Cowboys game doesn’t go to overtime I don’t lose our match up last week by two points. Potter is the Cowboys for all the reasons you think.(Not America’s team if that's what you got).

6 - Galt - Bengals - I had Galt pencilled in as the Bills on the list for a while since both have been to the Superbowl. The Bills famously lost four in a row, Galt has one less loss but same amount of wins. Zero. Unfortunately this year’s Bills and Galts team are not comparable. Like the Bengals the injuries are already taking their toll on each of these teams.

7 - Trey - 49ers - Both have not won in forever, both are an annual let down.

8 - Shain - Panthers - How did you basically work for the team for seven years yet I can not find a half decent picture of you in Panthers’ gear? I do have your Panther growl on tape for future use. Shain and the Panthers have been to the Superbowl twice and lost twice. They’re both in Charlotte. Tepper is kind of an asshole…Shain’s team is not 2025 Panthers bad. AJ Brown might be unless he is playing against me. Jake Ferguson has been and should continue to be one of the better waiver wire pick ups on the year. The next three weeks are huge for Shain as he gets Rex this week while Rex’s Falcons are on bye, a depleted Waivers team next week, and current bottom dweller Wood the next week.

9 - Waivers - Giants - David Tyree's helmet catch is the only comparison I could think of for how Waivers was able to win his championship. Pure luck some would say. Others would say it was Waiversing. You can safely drop James Conner. His career is over. Also, this message would be for everyone in the league. You have an IR spot. I suggest you use it. This team reminds me of the 2022 Panthers. You just feel bad that CMC is being wasted on this team.

10 - Wood - Saints - Did Wood ever actually look like Drew Brees? After losing the Taylor bowl for the fourth time in a row, a matchup this week with Waivers could have major Caddy bowl impacts down the road. How have you guys only played each other sixteen times? The Conley/Rose divisions skewed things for years but that is still incredible. Waivers leads the all time series 11-5.
