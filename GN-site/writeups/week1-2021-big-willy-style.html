2021 · Week 1

Big Willy Style

Week 1 has come and gone. The high highs and low lows of fantasy have bestowed upon us.

Fortunately for this group we did not even make it to kickoff Sunday before we had inner league fireworks. Afterwards I had decided that I was going to compare all of you to an ex girlfriend or a lady of the night from your past. I mean who else hasn’t heard a good “Madison just sucked my pecker, NOTHING ELSE” or an ole butthole Kelly joke in a while AMIRITE? But I would need an NDA signed before I put that out to the public. Also this is a big week for a few league members' lives, which will be tied into the new theme. I start the year by comparing everyone's team to Will Smith(2nd best black actor of all time) movies...

1) Men in Black(The original) - Team Potter - Men in Black is his best movie...Potter started the year out with a bang, pacing the league with a week 1 best 158.7 points(19~ points better than the next). I have a hard time writing/saying/thinking positive things about Potter so without the ability to erase his memory I have nothing else to say.

2) Enemy of the State - The Goatfather - Enemy of the League. I, Trey, a man of the people takes constant heat from other league members despite their disregard for constant attempts of betterment of the league.

3) The Pursuit of Happiness - Make Hud Great Again - Hud and Emily expect to welcome their daughter to the world this weekend. He will be spared from slander this week.

4) I Am Legend - Washington Smoothskins - Galt may be the best suited for a zombie apocalypse. Although some would argue that his bold decision making would be his downfall. Taking Najee SIXTH overall was as bold of a pick as we’ve ever had in this league. After some minor consultation and educational guessing one would think there was a great chance he would still be there at 15. Bold moves are made by legends. Time will tell if Galt made one.

5) Hitch - Roy Johnson in dis bish - Shain and Nicole are getting hitched this weekend. He is spared from any slander for the week since he will most likely not have a chance to read.

6) Independence Day - Mikedale Stoopkids - I wanted to give Waivers “Ali'' but he doesn’t float like a butterfly anymore and the only thing that stings is the loss he took to Potter last week. Also Ali is an all-time champion, something Waivers has yet to achieve. Independence Day is upon Waivers as his team appears to be a contender again but will he be able ward off the fantasy aliens of years past?

7) Six Degrees of Separation - Ball So Hard University - These are the losses that leave a long lasting burn. Poor ole T-Wood. Brandon Aiyuk. If Taylor had gone with Robby Anderson instead of Aiyuk he would be sitting in second place after week 1. If he had selected the other wide receiver on the 49ers he’d be in second place. If he hadn’t found out that TikTok is semi-acceptable softcore porn he’d still be unsticking the pages of his 2011 Victoria's Secret magazine before/during his morning shower. If he hadn’t found a spoon that doubled as cereal straw. Six Degrees of Separation.

8) Bad Boys - The Replacements - Since Waivers is lazy and did not do his annual team name rankings(can’t wait for him to say it was his back’s fault), I’ll chime in. Beginning the year with the team name “The Replacements” does not make much sense to me. What are they replacing? Over the past couple years Rex has changed from the footloose and fancy free ladies man Mike Lowrey to the more reserved family man Marcus Bennett. Currently riding a SEVEN game losing streak(Last Win Week 6 2020), Rex is hoping to bust the bad out of his boys sooner than later.

9) Hancock - Cobra Kai Strike First - After griping about the waiver wire order that was out of the commissioners hands last week, Ian finds himself in prime waiver wire position this week! I’m not Ian’s commissioner(got the shirt to prove it) so I am not sure why he was upset with me about ESPN’s ways. Best of luck with the second waiver wire pick this week E you earned it...

10) Wild Wild West - Cody's Fantasy Team - I am beginning to understand why there was no team name ranking as the effort level on the team names have hit a new low. Cody’s team tried equally as hard for him this week. Clod never saw himself as James West or Artemus Gilmore or even Dr Loveless but as General Dirtbath McGrath for “reasons”. Taking Henry over Cook/Kamara will likely haunt Clod for the year. (Disclaimer: this may or may not be my personal favorite Will Smith movie)
