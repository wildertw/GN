Theme Park Rides

Ten managers, ten rides, ranked off the same list. Two launched coasters up front and a set of pedal cars in the back.

156.1WEEK 3 HIGH · REX OVER CLOD
67.2WEEK 3 LOW · SHAIN, 0-3
5WOOD'S WINNING STREAK, LONGEST SINCE 2019

Three weeks in and the park's finally sorted itself out. Wood and I are 3-0 and the two of us have not played each other yet, which feels like something the league office should have looked at. Rex woke up and dropped 156.1 on the defending champ. Waivers and Lee both got off the mat for their first wins. And down at the bottom, Shain and Clod are 0-3 and a combined 8-46 against the field.

Last week I laid out what a 2-0 and 0-2 start is worth historically. Here's where that leaves us: the two of us at 3-0 are sitting at 61.9 percent to make the playoffs, the four teams at 2-1 are a coin flip at 50, the three at 1-2 are down to 27.6, and the two 0-3 teams are looking at 20 percent, which is 5 out of 25 in seventeen years. It's not over for Shain and Clod. It's just that the ride operator has already started walking toward the lever.

This week's theme is theme park rides, ranked off a best-and-worst list of ride types, so where you landed says as much as the number next to your name. Week 4 previews are at the bottom as usual, head to head history, the margin marks and the position comparison for all five games.

03 / THE WRITE-UP

The park, ranked

The best of the park

Rides people line up for on purpose.

1

Wood Launched Roller Coaster

3-0 · Power 90.2 · 371.6 PF · Won 124.5–107.5

No lift hill, no slow climb, just zero to sixty before you've finished getting your hands up. Wood beat Potter and that's five straight wins going back to last year, his first five game winning streak since 2019, and he's been first in the power rankings two weeks running. Gibbs and JSN put up 68 of his 124.5, which is the launch: two enormous bursts and some flat track to coast on. CWCaleb Williams injury could have been the thing that killed the momentum. Instead he dropped PMPatrick Mahomes into the seat and kept going. He's second in the league in points at 371.6 and this is no longer a fluke or a soft schedule story, it's the fastest thing in the park.

Best ride in the park. Nobody's arguing anymore.

2

Trey Log Flume

3-0 · Power 87.0 · 342.7 PF · Won 121.6–73.8

Slow climb, nice view, one satisfying splash at the end, and everybody gets off happy and slightly damp. I'm 3-0 with the fewest points against in the league at 259.0 and the highest floor at 109.2, and I still have not had one week anybody would describe as thrilling. Lamar, Cook and JLJeremiyah Love all landed between 19 and 21 and the Vikings defense chipped in 17. That's the whole ride: gentle, steady, and you're dry again by the time you reach the midway. Then I started DSDalton Schultz at tight end for 4.5 while MGMatthew Golden put up 18.5 on my bench, which is the part where the water gets in your shoe. Beating Ian is what I'll take, since I'm now 5-11 against him lifetime. Hud's next and he's 15-11 on me.

Not the best ride here. Best line-to-payoff ratio in the park.

3

Rex Hypercoaster

2-1 · Power 80.5 ▲2 · 420.8 PF · Won 156.1–93.6

Massive drops, real speed, and that moment where you come out of your seat and your stomach stays at the top. Rex has gone 170.2, 94.5 and 156.1, the widest three week range in the league, and he leads everybody in points, with the closest being wood who he leads by 49. His power record is 21-6, the best in the league. BBBrock Bowers finally got healthy enough to play and looked the part of the TE1 with 22.6 and Bijan went for 34.3. The best part: he benched TLTrevor Lawrence, started Tyler Shough, and Shough gave him 23.8. You do not make that decision unless you like the feeling of falling. He gets Shain this week, a series he leads 11-9.

Highest peak in the park. Also the longest way down.

4

Potter Wooden Roller Coaster

2-1 · Power 75.7 ▼2 · 349.4 PF · Lost 107.5–124.5

Built decades ago, rattles the whole way, and still rides better than half the steel in the park. Potter's first loss came against the hottest team in the league and he still put up 107.5 without PNPuka Nacua, who has now missed three straight and is turning into a permanent piece of the structure. Burrow went for 22.5, Henry 21.4, and the thing just kept clattering along. He dropped two spots, which says more about Rex and me than it does about him, and he's still fourth in points. He gets Lee this week in the most lopsided series in the league: Potter leads 11-7 with a +280.3 point differential, five blowouts, and a 225.4 hung on Lee somewhere in there.

Old, loud, and it'll still throw you out of your seat.

The ones you ride because you're already here

Fine in small doses. Nobody plans a day around them.

5

Ian Wild Mouse Coaster

2-1 · Power 63.4 ▼1 · 277.6 PF · Lost 73.8–121.6

Never gets above thirty miles an hour and still feels like it's coming off the track at every corner. Ian finally started DADavante Adams and got 17.2, and that's the week JJJustin Jefferson put up 4.2 and JTJonathan Taylor 8.2. 73.8 is his worst week of the year by 23 and it ended the undefeated run. Here's the part that should worry him: 2-1 with a 10-17 power record, a +6.3 luck score that's the best in the league, and an expected win percentage of 37. Every hairpin has gone his way so far. KPKyle Pitts has scored 3 points in three weeks combined, which at this point is a structural issue and not a slump.

Jerky, slow, and somehow still on the rails.

6

Waivers Boomerang Shuttle Coaster

1-2 · Power 60.4 ▲1 · 347.7 PF · Won 115.2–67.2

It runs the whole course forward, then does the exact same thing backward, and you end up right where you started. That's been Waivers all year: 347.7 points, fourth in the league, a 17-10 power record, and a 1-2 record to show for it. He finally got his win and it wasn't close, 115.2 to 67.2, and he did it without a big week from JAJosh Allen, who managed 16.8. GWGarrett Wilson went for 21.7 and CMC 19.6. The Eagles defense scored -1 after giving him 2 the week before, so his defense has taken points off the board two weeks running, which is fixable with four minutes of effort on a Saturday. He gets Clod this week, a series he trails 9-10 and in which Clod has won three straight.

Same ride, backwards, again.

7

Hud The Rotor

1-2 · Power 57.7 ▼1 · 327.4 PF · Lost 108.1–121.7

They spin the drum until you're stuck to the wall, then the floor drops out and there's nothing you can do about any of it. Hud has had 419.5 points thrown at him in three weeks, the most in the league by 63 and 92 more than he's scored himself. 108.1 would have beaten two teams this week and he drew the one that woke up. The one thing he can control he didn't: JWJaylen Warren put up 19.1 on his bench, JAJordan Addison 17.5 and WRWan'Dale Robinson 15.2 while BHBreece Hall gave him 7 and Amon-Ra 9.9. That's 51.8 sitting on the bench in a 13.6-point loss. A 15-12 power record says this is better than a 1-2 team. Hud's fantasy season is riding on the thigh of BHBreece Hall. His decision to not make a single waiver claim as of this writing has many league members scratching their heads.

Pinned to the wall. Floor's already gone.

8

Lee Bumper Cars

1-2 · Power 52.7 ▲1 · 293.0 PF · Won 121.7–108.1

For two weeks Lee was the guy stuck in the corner getting hit from three sides. This week he got loose and hit somebody. 121.7 is his best week of the year and it snapped the five game losing streak he brought into the season, which means the record for fewest of those stays in Waivers' lap. The quarterback situation is its own attraction: JDJaxson Dart in Week 1, JDJayden Daniels in Week 2, BPBrock Purdy for 31.2 in Week 3. Three weeks, three drivers, and the third one was the best. Kittle went 23.2, DLDrake London 23.9 and Harrison Mevis kicked 16. He still has the worst luck score in the league at -5.6.

Finally out of the corner. Straight into Potter.

The back lot

The ones your stomach remembers and not fondly.

9

Shain Tilt-A-Whirl

0-3 · Power 33.2 ▼1 · 255.9 PF · Lost 67.2–115.2

You never know which way the car's going to spin and neither does the guy running it. 67.2 is the lowest score of the week, the lowest of his season, and his third straight loss. It was his lowest score since he put up 73.9 in Week 11 of 2024. The $46 he spent out of his $100 FAAB on Jalen Coker returned 2.8 points. DKDalton Kincaid, the guy he traded ZFZay Flowers to get, also went 2.8. The 49ers defense scored -2 and MWMichael Wilson put up 20.4 on the bench, more than any three of his starters combined. He's 5-22 against the field with an expected win percentage of 18.5, both dead last, and he's already burned through three quarterbacks and three tight ends. Here's the cruel part: the all-time Week 4 high score in this league is Shain's, 179.7.

Every seat's a different ride. None of them are good.

10

Clod Pedal-Powered Aerial Cars

0-3 · Power 30.9 · 238.6 PF · Lost 93.6–156.1

All the effort in the park and you move about four miles an hour, sweating, above a parking lot, while everyone below you watches. The defending champion is 0-3 with a 3-24 power record and 238.6 points, fewest in the league. His ceiling for the season is 93.6, the lowest high score anybody has, which means he hasn't had one good week yet, just three bad ones of varying severity. He finally cut bait at quarterback and JGJared Goff gave him 19.2, easily the best he's gotten from the spot, and SBSaquon Barkley went for 8.5 in a week where KWKyren Williams put up 18.8.

Pedal harder. Nothing happens.
