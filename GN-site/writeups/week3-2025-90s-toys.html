2025 · Week 3

90s Toys

We are basically already a quarter of the way through the regular season. Some of you may want to give up already but if Shain and the Panthers have brought us hope! Well not me but, the fireworks were booming in Charlotte as Shain finally broke his record tying eleven game losing streak beating me, just as I predicted last week. I started to think the player that has hurt me most in fantasy over the last 3-4 years has been Jalen Hurts. Owning Saquon is just the cherry on top of it all. Cheaters. The tush push is stupid, and I don’t want to hate it but they make it hard not to when its pushing my fantasy tush.

Anyways, I had been working on this for a bit and it still isn’t 100% correct but in the Weekly Tale of Tape will now include who your top scoring player against your opponent has been since 2018. We’re still ironing some things out, like Ian’s top scoring tight end was Taysom Hill not Robby Anderson with the same amount of points scored(17.4).

This week’s theme is 90’s toy. I am pretty sure I have done this one before but I can not find it.

  1. Hud - Socker Boppers - Hud is the only undefeated team left in the league. Hud is on a 9 game winning streak, a personal best and tied for 4th best in league history with Wood. Hud has been Sock Bopping everyone he has played so far and his team is only getting stronger. This is also the first time in league history Hud has started 3-0.
  2. Ian - Big Wheel - I know the Big Wheel didn’t come out in the 90s but everyone wanted a Big Wheel growing up and Ian’s got what everyone wants, two RBs competing for the RB1 overall spot. At this point I think Ian is just big dogging us. He has the most points scored. He has the highest power record. If/when Justin Jefferson gets going, I don’t see how this juggernaut is going to slow down.
  3. Rex - Rock 'Em Sock 'Em Robots - Rock Em Sock Em Rex has a good ring to it. The hits keep coming but Rex has been fighting back. Losing Mike Evans was one for the bad guys but luckily for Rex Jordan Mason and George Pickens gain value thanks in part to injuries to Aaron Jones and CeeDee Lamb. Drake London has almost been as bad as Brian Thomas Jr through three weeks. Rex currently has a three game winning streak in the heated Taylor bowl. Both teams have only ever allowed a three game winning streak to the other Taylor.
  4. Cody - Super Soaker 200 - I’ll be the first to say, I think Clod’s team ambushed us. I did not see this fire power coming from them. Snagging JSN and Garrett Wilson in the 4th/5th rounds has been some of the best value one could ask for. I’m sure Clod thought this write up would include a story about the Harris Teeter parking one evening but it will not be included.
  5. Potter - The Game of Perfection - “Some kids just wanna see stuff explode.” was the tagline for this game. It matched immediately. Despite being second overall in points, Potter is just 1-2. Let this be a reminder to you all that this is a game of perfection. I know it has been grinding his gears to end. I don’t have all the data set but Alvin Kamara’s 150.3 points scored against me is absolutely absurd and is likely the most by any player against one team. I hate that guy.
  6. Shain - Stretch Armstrong - As noted above, Shain’s team was stretched about as far they could go. Finally and predictably going off last week. Tush Push here, AJ Brown there and the Eagles game was a dream come true for Shain. Jeanty’s stat line of having more yards after contact than actual yards has to mean good things are coming right? The last guy that went on an 11 games losing streak went on the win nine* in a row. Just saying.
  7. Waivers - Moon Shoes - That was a brutal, brutal Sunday for Dirty Mike and the boys as

Waivers wishes he could go back in time and put some Moon Shoes on CeeDee and Conner. Along with having the most points scored against him through three weeks. Waivers your highest scoring WR against Ian is DeAndre Hopkins with the same amount. I’ll have this fixed this week. I know this disappoints you.

  1. Trey - Skip It - I know you guys will Skip It anyways and I feel as though I continually hit myself in the ankle, but if my team can pull together I’m gonna look damn cool doing it.
  2. Lee - Crayola Stampers Markers - Lets draw a picture, I’ll start with the happy stamps, Lee currently has a three game winning streak against Shain. Jayden Daniels is hopeful to play as of this writing. The bad stamps, Lee has failed to break 80 two out of the three weeks. Its a long season and that is a small sample size. Chase Brown has been bad. The hope stamps, Lee has had a top tier WR core, are they better than say Potter and Huds?
  3. Wood - Rubik’s Cube - Wood’s team has improved their scoring each week and even picked up his first win last week against Galt. His team has been pieced together and I think he might be starting to figure it out. Brock Bowers has been good but not the world beater everyone thought he would be. There’s a universe out there that he’s on Waivers team right now. Hud is currently undefeated so maybe this is the year Indiana Jones is a top 5 QB and Quinton Johnson is a top 5 WR.
