2025 · Week 5

True Colors

Let me start with this and this is the first, last, and only time I will be addressing it. This is a FANTASY football league. There will be jokes, jabs, gabs, pranks, ball busting, and all the above with the boys. If you have a personal issue with someone in this league, don’t bring that shit in here. I understand things get heated and we may occasionally say something that we do not really mean or that can be taken the wrong way but it has become more of an issue this year than ever before. We are all in our mid to late thirties. Lets fucking act like it. I will oversee league matters and that's it. This isn’t communist Russia. I was not planning on writing this week, but as the great commissioner that I am, I will. There are quite a few noteworthy stats to check out that I should have pointed out last week but missed.

1a) Hud - We are in unchartered territory for Hud. This is only the third time Hud has started 2-0(2009 & 2021 as well). After setting the league record(spoiler: more on this later) for consecutive losses with 11, he has now won a personal best of 8 in a row. Some will say Hud has played the weakest schedule thus far and they would be right. But as coach Boone would put it, it doesn’t matter if it is by a foot or a mile, a win is a win. Winnings winning! Does Omarian Hampton look like the biggest bust in the draft? Maybe and Huds season will likely hinge on whether or not he turns it around. It is also the only reason why he is ranked as 1a and not 1 outright.

1b) Cody - Did not see this coming. After draft night, I said Clod’s team would likely be a boom or bust team. I have changed my stance. After almost being taken advantage of in the offseason by rival Potter, the fantasy gods showed their might by awarding Cody with a Baker Mayfield game winning drive on Potter’s Texans D/ST. Pulling off his second career win by less than a point against Potter. His only other win by less than a point came against Potter in 2016. Cody has finished a league leading six times (No one else has more than twice) in 4th place and is on track to be there again.

3) Ian - Here we go again. I knew Coach would not let his team stay down for long. If James Cook keeps this pace, the rest of us might as well stop trying now. E currently sits with a league best 16-2 power record. Meaning if he had played anyone else's schedule besides Potters and my schedules he would be undefeated. Good coaching outweighs good luck…

4) Potter - The man just wants to watch the world burn. And I despise him for it. I was surprised as any when I did not hear any whining after losing in the final seconds of Monday Night Football. If Potter finds a way to miss the playoffs this year, we will never hear the end of the Bowers-CMC-Kamara drama he created for himself. How do you always get Brandon Aubrey? Potter is only 88.9 points away from joining Trey in the 22K points club.

5) Trey - Apparently Brian Thomas Jr became a huge pussy over the offseason and it will cost me my playoff hopes. Sigh…

6) Lee - Lee is probably more of 5b than 6 but I didn’t want to make that a theme for this ranking or future rankings. The question everyone wants to ask right now: How bad is Jayden's knee? Lee didn’t deem any of the waiver wire quarterbacks worthy of a bid or add as of this writing so he must have some insider knowledge the rest don't have. Tyler Warren=Stud. Although he won their last matchup, Galt faces his toughest opponent this week in T-Wood, whom he has a 6-12 all time record.

7) Rex - This seems low. Rex has already won the team name award for the year. If you don’t get the reference, long-time friend of the league and future NYT best selling author

Lee Shaver has published his first romance novel. It is EVERYTHING that you would think. Here is a link with one of my favorite quotes from the book: “ Vomit mouth and Everything “. Rex’s team has gotten off to a slow start but I do not expect that to continue. Unfortunately for Rex he plays Waivers this week, who he has a personal worst winning percentage against at 38%.

8) Waivers - You can interchange anyone in spots 4-8 right now. We will know more in a few weeks. It’s fair to say the top 3 have separated themselves through two games. There is too much consistency in Waiver’s team (at this time) for them to stay this close to the bottom for too long.

9) Shain - I am going to text you and apologize before posting this. And I do not want to even put this out there as I know what karma it is going to cause me but I did not become the greatest commissioner of all time worrying about karma. So here it is: Shain has now tied the league record Hud took from him just a year ago, with ELEVEN losses in a row. Week 4 a year ago, Shain was looking at 3-1 record looking to make a playoff push and possibly his first ever five game winning streak. This should be a stark reminder to all of you how quickly the tides can turn in this game. I write all of this fully knowing Shain is going to beat me this week.

10) Wood - 2017-2019 Wood finished 2nd, 1st, and 3rd respectively. Since 2020 he has finished 8th > 7th > 5th > 9th > 9th. On average that's by far the worst in the league. Yikes. Things aren’t looking much better after losing Toe Burrow but who knows, maybe Indiana Jones will give his team the boost it badly needs. I heard Waivers was willing to trade CMC for Bowers. If I was you I would knock the tires on that one. I write this knowing that he will not read this but it is there for public record keeping.
