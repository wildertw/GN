Half way power rankings Halo 3 style. Every team will be compared to a Halo weapon:

1. Waivers - Sniper Rifle - The crown jewel of Halo 3, sneaking at a distance and double-zooming straight onto the head of an weak enemy(Rex) has to be one of the greatest feelings in gaming. Waivers sits at the highest point of the map sniping away. Can anyone bring him down?

2. Ian - Rocket Launcher - Huge, intimidating, and powerful, capable of dealing massive damage in a single shot/week. Ians team seems to have locked on and its never good when a rocket launcher is locked in.

3. Potter - Spartan Laser - It might only have five shots, but each of them is pretty much guaranteed to destroy its target. Poubters team may not have 5 shots but he has two that are capable of knocking a team out in any given week.

4. Cody - Energy Sword - Overheard Clod on the phone ordering one of these

5. Trey - Gravity Hammer - The reason you’ve never seen a member of the Covenant wielding a Gravity Hammer is because this is another of the weapons specific to the Brutes.

6. Shain - Beam Rifle - Technically, the Beam Rifle is as proficient in headshots as the Sniper Rifle. Shains team on paper is almost as equally as strong as Waivers in regards to Points Scored(707.6/689.7) and Points Allowed (677.2/669.9). Yet they’re separated by 2 wins. Shain’s team will need to overtake Waivers perch, a daunting task.

7. Wood - Assault Rifle - The standard weapon in Halo 3, There isn’t a lot to say either way for this weapon as it falls into just about every ‘average’ bracket possible. Much like Wood’s team.

8. Galt - Needler - One of the most famous weapons in Halo history is the Needler. An enemy shoots you and you’re unable to evade its lock-on mechanic? Furious. Can Lee Needle his way into the playoffs?

9. Rex - Shotgun - It was very clear that the shotgun was a powerful, if short-range, weapon. Unfortunately for Rex, he has been running around with the shotty in the large, open map. Rex is playing the guy running around with the shotgun trying to end everyone else's kill streaks for the rest of the season.

10. Hud - Plasma Rifle - When it was first introduced, the Plasma Rifle was a rather powerful weapon. By the time of Halo 3, it was one of the weakest guns in the game and forced you to recoil in pain after over-shooting. I could make several jokes about Hud’s fantasy team but I’m running out of material.
