Madden

Week 10: Home stretch with Madden

It’s been a while. Going into the final three weeks we have eight teams still fighting for a playoff spot. Realistically its probably more like six butt for entertainment purposes lets say eight. I have several topics I put in my phone notes throughout the year but they have to make sense for the time. I was going to do Thanksgiving foods with Waivers being Turkey. Dry and disappointing turkey. But I couldn’t kick a man while he’s down on vacation. Wood was going to be canned cranberry sauce. Instead I chose Madden cover athletes.

  1. Lamar Jackson Madden 21 - Mild Mike and Pals - I had a few good knocks on how Waivers and Lamar can not win the big game despite repeated regular season success. Again Waivers is to blame himself yet again. Another week, another big injury. Losing Cooper Kupp might be the one to put Waivers down. You could say his team bombed. This isn’t the first time an American was bombed while in Hawaii. We won’t even bring up him dishonoring Japan…
  2. Mike Vick Madden 04 - You Can't Jump with JJ - Arguably the greatest video game athlete of our generation. Growing up, whenever your pal got the Falcons in Madden 04 you immediately thought “Shit”. Whenever one plays Galt the thought is about the same. But yet again, neither one of these guys have ever been able to win when it matters most.
  3. Rob Gronkowski Madden 17 - Throwing Bombs and BangingMoms - Gronk smash. Rex Smash. Both guys are known for their passion for smashing things. Gronk was the best tight end of his time and has passed the torch onto Kelce. Clearly Rex knows a thing or two about tight ends.
  4. Ray Lewis Madden 02 - Ball So Hard University - If only T-Sizzle got a Madden cover it would be a perfect fit for the team name. Woods' team is playing defense at an unprecedented level. Yet to even allow 1000 points on the season and forty points from the next closest. Woods’ years of playing NCAA 14 as a D Coordinator have finally started to pay off.
  5. John Madden Madden 23 - A center for Ants - In recent years, I have found myself asking how does E do it? Year in and year out, I look at Ian’s team after the draft or even at midseason and think “Damn E, what are you doing? Team looks like doodoo”. Yet, the past few years Ian has found himself in contention with the best team. I have come to the conclusion that it’s coaching. Ian is out here outcoaching all of us at Belichickian level. Wouldn’t be surprised if the Colts call him next.
  6. Donovan McNabb Madden 06 - Mike's Cheesefakes - If only Cam could have edged out Richard Sherman in Madden 15 this one could have been a little easier. Despite being ravaged by injuries in 04 the McNabb led Eagles still found a way to the superbowl. Can Shain find a way to do the same?
  7. Brett Favre Madden 09 - Team Rectal Discharge - (deleted)
  8. Peyton Hillis Madden 12 - That ONE Time - Fun fact: Peyton Hillis has as many Madden covers as Hud does playoff appearances.
  9. Richard Sherman Madden 15 - Keenan Allen Is Elite - I’m sure Potter thought he was going to be Tom or even Mahomes. Truthfully I wanted to make Potter Antonio Brown but I am pretty sure he’s going to jump on the anti-semtic train at some point and I do not want to upset him. Both are notorious for complaining, being loud, and chain yanking(in their own way). Luckily we only have to hear from them on the sidelines.
  10. Dante Culpepper Madden 02 - Leather Cheerios - Despite being loaded with top end talent, Dante and I just can't figure out a way to get it done. I blame coaching, the offense, and the defense. But mostly the special teams.
