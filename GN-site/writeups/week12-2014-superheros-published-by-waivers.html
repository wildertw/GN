Superheros(published by Waivers)

Week 12 Write up Superheros

Anyway here is you're final regular season league note.

Mark Potter - The Incredible Hulk - So I'm sure you all can see the similarities between Potter and The Hulk. Mild-mannered Bruce Banner was subjected to Gamma radiation which caused him to go into uncontrollable fits of rage when his heart rate got too high. Mark Potter was subjected to alcohol which caused him to go into uncontrollable fits of rage when his heart rate got too high. But this comparison goes further than that, in that Potter’s team is damn near indestructible when at full strength. Start taking away a player or two, and his team is just like any other. He is the favorite when his team is at full strength, but a lot can happen in 4 weeks.

Josh Hudson – Superman - Superman is an alien to our planet and Hud just plain looks like an alien. That’s enough similarity for me. Superman is the have-it-all superhero due to what I suspect is lazy writing. Here is a reenactment of a typical day for Superman’s writers.

Writer 1: So how is Superman going to save the kittens from the burning building?

Writer 2: He can just pick it up right? And go in and find them? Because he is invulnerable?

Writer 1: No no no that will never work, the kittens will die real fast he doesn't have enough time to find them.

Writer 2: He can use his X-Ray vision to find them quickly.

Writer 1: His X-Ray vision?

Writer 2: Yes. He has that I swear.

Writer 1: Brilliant Johnson, you've done it again.

Writer 2: Thank you, pass me some of that easy money being made.

Point is, Hud's team is strong in all positions, minus maybe tight end. We'll call that his kryptonite.

Trey Wilder – Captain America - Trey is like Captain America in that he is the leader of the Avengers and Trey is the commissioner of us Degenerates. Captain America is the somewhat lame superhero that become the leader because noone else really wants to do any of the shit work managing a group of superhumans. Everybody likes Captain America yet the more popular superheroes relentlessly pick on him. Regardless, he is our leader, defending us with his shield from certain league self-destruction. Here's to you league commissioner, real man of genius.

Lee Galt – Spiderman - Let's get the low hanging fruit out of the way, Lee likes Mary Jane. Welcome to league Galt, you may have officially lost your rookie status. I can't tell you how pissed I was that fateful Monday night, until I realized I was just a footnote in the Saga of Galt's Revenge. He has taken me and Rex out of the playoffs and he is on to you all next. Good luck. Spiderman is the youngest superhero on this list, but he manages to hang with the big dogs. After 4 years, Lee finally made it to the playoffs and looks to do some damage to the vets.

Michael Waivers – Iron Man - Handsome, charming, witty, and brilliant. Built a suit that allows him to hang with real superheros from limited resources, just as I built a team to hang with the playoff contenders from limited resources. Got burned in the end, but it was a good fight. Second choice for me was Green Lantern because just as he can create anything with his ring, I can pull anything out of my ass.

Taylor Hardee – Thor - Big and beastly with a commanding roar and a penchant for taking off his shirt, Rex is Thor. You can see the jealousy in his eyes when he encounters the bouncer Thor because he claimed that awesome nickname. Thor is an incredibly powerful superhero who never really gets listed amongst the top guys. He is a veritable god that still just a notch below our favorites.

Cody Clodfelter – The Thing - Clod is always Rock Hard.

Taylor Wood – Storm - Storm has all the cool powers in the world, but can never really seem to use them effectively. I mean she can control weather, but all she ever fucking does with it is make it windy, rainy, or cold. Fucking shoot lightning bolts out of your tits or something for god's sake. Throw some damn tornadoes out of your pussy. Also she could save so many more lives if she went around preventing natural disasters instead of teaching at some mutant school or sucking off Professor X. Don't just float and make it windy that shit sucks. Just like Wood's year.

Shain Collins – Robin - Sorry Shain, you are the perennial side kick. Cam is your Batman and you are he sexy little Robin. I don't have any data to back it up, but I feel like you always field a good team and get horrible luck with match ups. Maybe one day you'll become Nightwing (look it up nerds) and destroy everything but until then just be happy you aren't receiving the Marlowe...again.

Ian Baro – Aquaman - The shittiest superhero. Damn Ian I feel bad for you now, but I'm going to feel real bad Sunday. I hope you know what you're in for. The Unveiling is an ancient tradition dating all the way back to 2013. The last person to receive the Marlowe was never seen again. Good luck.

Fuck Jimmy Graham.
