2023 · Week 3

No Ceilings

Year 15. Fifteen years ago in our dirty living room(Waiver’s bedroom) the league was born. Redzone being born in 2009 makes me consider changing the league name or incorporating it in some way. I’ll take any ideas. I’m pretty sure that year was the closest we came to having an in person draft. Maybe 6 people in attendance? After year 2, Wood would lock himself in his room or even leave the house on draft night. I asked myself what would we be listening to on draft night 2009? Only one of the best mixtapes to ever drop, No Ceilings. I am debating if I want to rank the teams by standings, Wilder Power Rankings™, or where I think the team will finish. I’ll save predictions for the Week 6 write up. Wilder Power Rankings™ it is.

Shain - Run this town - Shain can feel it in the air, screaming for Tyreek to score every Sunday, everywhere. He’s addicted to the thrill, squeakin by with two wins of less than 3 points before blowing Waivers out and destroying his will. Shain is running this league this league through three weeks(AYEEE AAYe aAYee AYE aye). Shain plays Cody next week who is the only team he has a winning record(7-6) against. Huh?

Ian - No Ceilings - I have slim recollections of this song but Ian’s team is doing what they do. Sitting comfortably in second place. One week from possibly getting Jonathon Taylor and Cooper Kupp back. If Ians training staff is anything like his coaching staff, this is over. Currently Ian has QB2, RB3, RB12, RB15, WR3, WR5, & TE5. That is scary considering the two guys coming back have been the top of their position over the past 2 years. On the opposite end of the spectrum from Shain, Ian has a winning record(greater than 50%) against everyone in the league except for Galt(3-9).

Trey - o - Swag Surf - You think we gon' do our thing? Well, ain't it sunny in the summer? Injuries held us back this past week.

Potter - Ice Cream - Much of Potter's team entered the league when “Ice Cream” came out. He might have the only team in the league that has an average age that is older than the league average age. Regardless, they're rolling right now. Can they hold up?

Lee - Watch my Shoes - Galt said watch my shoes and griddied by the first two weeks playing the lowest and second lowest scorers in consecutive weeks only to run into Ian’s nuclear week 3. Two matches against me over the next three weeks with Waivers sandwiched in between will have major play implications.

Rex - D.O.A. - Death of auction budget. The world is overwhelmed with Swift drama but it was Deandre breaking poor Rex’s heart. Rex has heard that he blew his wad too early before, but this may take the cake. Is spending half your budget on a running back that through 195 carries has never had a carry over 20 yards savvy? No, but picking up Kyren Williams certainly was and it would be the best free agent pick up thus far if it was not for…

Waivers - Puka Her Face - Oh, oh-oh, oh, Oh, oh-oh, oh P-P-P-Puka Nucua P-P-P-Puka Nucua. No name stealing Waivers! I am sure you have tried to change your name to Pukachu but ESPN banned it. Waivers has the most average head to head record in league history. With many of his records against hovering around 50% so no fun stats…for now.

Hud - Wayne on me - Hud should be compwayning about his underperforming team. Maybe all his team needed was a get right game against me per usual. Kamara coming back this week is a huge plus. A match against rival Potter(3-14 lifetime, 2-0 last year) looming next week followed by two of the next four against Wood could make or break Huds' season.

Wood - Wasted - Rookie lifestyle , might not make it. Reeling in his first win of the year and his fourth win in a row against Rex got the whole clique wasted. **team name change alert** For the first time since changing from “Taco Hell” to “Ball so hard University” in 2012, Wood has changed his name. He is 1-0 with the new team name.

Cody - Throw it in the bag - I tried to find something to say here but there isn't much to get excited about. Ekeler over CMC was a wild move but that isn’t the reason ole Clod is sitting in last. Looming matches against Shain and Ian(Ian has won the last 5 against Clod by an average of ~30 points) Clod is looking most likely to be on the bag caddying. It is always darkest before dawn…
