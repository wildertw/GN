2020 · Week 7

Beach music

We are entering the prime “it’s football season” time of year. Take a minute. Stare at your team. Inhale. Smell that? It’s ok if you don’t. Some of you(looking at you Hud and Wood) have basically squandered your playoff chances already. For those of us that can still smell your playoff chances, embrace it. It is the most wonderful and tragic time of year. And the best part is we are only at the halfway point. Cody is leading the charge and as an ode to him, the rankings will be beach music teams.

  1. The Embers-I Love Beach Music - Cody’s fantasy team - I have to eat my words. Cody’s drafting Henry over the other top backs was a world class move. There’s no other kind of back in the league that gives him quite the thrill. I am beginning to think he loves his team as much as he loves beach music. And he loves beach music, always has and always will. You know that you do.
  2. The Tams-Be Young, Be Foolish, Be Happy - Washington Smoothskins - Lee got his guitar out on draft night and serenaded us all with this tune. Perhaps we were not listening but he sang his song! Drafting four rookies and four second year players, Lee has the youngest team in league history. Maybe. I didn’t actually look it up. He has had one of the strongest teams on the year and is looking to lock in a playoff spot.
  3. “Shama Lama Ding Dong”-Band of Oz/Otis Day & the Knights - Roy johnson is dis bish - SSHHHHHAIIINNNNNAAAAAAA LLLAAMMMAA baby DDINNGGGGG DONGGGG hey hey. If Shain searched the whole world, he’d never never find a stud QB he’d love as much as his Brady boo. Rama rama ding dong. Oh oh oh oh.
  4. “Sixty Minute Man”-Bill Ward & his Dominoes - It’s Still Baseball Season - Sixty-minute man, sixty-minute man. Look here girls I'm telling you now. They call me "Lovin' Dan" . I rock 'em, roll 'em all night long. I'm a sixty-minute man. And if you don’t believe me, I promise girl I will last longer than Christian McCaffrey can. Sixty minute man.
  5. “Carolina Girls-Chairman of the Board” - Goat Goons - Best in the world.
  6. 7. “Under the Boardwalk”-The Drifters - Mikedale Stoopkids AND Cobra Kai strike first - For the first time in league history we have a tie among the WPI (Wilder Power Index). Sure there have been ties in the past but through six games, one measly points separate these two. ONE. That is probably a missed field goal somewhere. The unfortunate thing is they are sitting under the boardwalk, falling out of the playoff sun, sailing out to sea.
  7. “Sand in my Shoes”-Steve Jarrell - The Replacements - Sand isn’t only in Rex’s shoes if you know what I mean.
  8. “Bless Your Heart”-Chairman of the Board - Make Hud Great Again - Being that this is a great song, everyone from the south knows “bless your heart” is not a compliment. This comparison is not a compliment. Huds team had looked to have turned the corner but injuries and lack of a waiver wire presence have overtaken. Bless your heart hud.
  9. “Gone Fishin”-Chairman of the Board - “Ball So Hard University” - This one is pretty self explanatory, even if Wood doesn’t fish. Well, not in water anyways.
