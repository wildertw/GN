Greenville Natives

Sunday Sunday Sunday…preview

Tanking for Caleb vs Dirty Mike and the Boys

Perennial bottom-feeder Mark Potter takes on the struggling reigning champion in a fight to see who still has a shot at a playoffs and who is going to worry each week about having to be someone’s personal caddy. Thursday night gave us no good indicator of who is going to win the matchup and the deciding factor could well reside on whether CMC is going to suit up Monday night. Bye week hurts both teams, but will Tanking for Caleb’s choice of Raheem Mostert over Jeff Wilson Jr payoff? We all certainly hope not.

Let Galt Griddy * vs. Easy Pickens

The broken leg of the Brown’s Nick Chubb was a devastating blow, but Galt keeps on Griddying with an easy matchup vs. the appropriately named 1-5 Easy Pickens. Three 2-4 teams watch with anticipation rooting for the Griddy to be in full effect Sunday to keep the dirt clod in the last place position.

Isiah RUH ROW vs. OverCooked

Don’t look now, but the all time leading loser is on a hot streak and looks to take down Taylor “I start rapists at QB” Wood to solidify his position in the playoff race. With 3 players exceeding expectations on the Thursday night game, it looks like Coach Hudson is going to be getting a non-consensual happy ending courtesy of OverCooked.

Anita B. Jainow vs. Leather Cheerios

Leather Cheerios has half his starters on bye or out, so it looks like Anita B. Jainow is going to get that B. Jainow. With a sad showing from Calvin “I took the Under” Ridley, Trey may be breaking out those knee pads unless those San Franciscan boys can take advantage of the injuries to CMC and Deebo.

Waiver’s Game of the Week: It Reeks in Here vs Who’s my Caddie

The leading scorer and best team in the league takes on REX and plans to show him Who’s his Daddy. Both teams lose their waiver wire heroes and will be forced to rely on their drafted players to crush the opposition. Luckily, Shayne drafted that jet engine of a human being, Tyreek Hill, but it seems rex may be able to mitigate the freak with the man feeding him the ball. If the shortest coach in the league manages to win here, he will take his place among the top tier teams and level the playing field for everyone heading into the playoff hunt.
