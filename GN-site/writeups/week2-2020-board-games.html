2020 · Week 2

Board Games

You think this is a game?!

Two weeks in, ten to go. Since most of you are playing like this year like F'n children, I will be (loosely) comparing your teams to children's board games.

P.S. Before I start, I do have one request. I request that we keep all political talk out of fantasy football. It'll get shoved down our throats for the next six weeks as it is, lets keep this as a safe zone.

1. Working Man Blues - Hungry Hungry Hippos - I'll let you use your imagination as to this comparison. Yes its that.

2. Mikedale Stoop Boys - Chess/Chinese Checkers - WHAT ARE Y'ALL DOING?! How does one of the top teams in the league add a top tier wideout AND get one of the most desired waivers wire pick ups. Touche Waivers, out here playing 3-D chess...(EDIT: I had a good line about Waivers and Chinese Checkers, something about a slant but the incompetence of the league caused me to lose it).

3. Whats Cooking Whats Cooking - Yahtzee - Despite thinking Ian might have been playing pocket Yahtzee during the draft, he rolled the dice on several of his draft picks, and they keep hitting. Only time will tell if he continues to have the hot hand.

4. On to the Next - Life - The game simulates a person's travels through his or her life, from college to retirement, with jobs, marriage, and possible children along the way. If only it was that easy, right Potter?

5. The Goatfather - Monopoly - The ultimate game of testing friendships. Bout to start building hotels on all yall.

6. One Chub In Paris - Battleship - Players alternate turns calling "shots" at the other player's ships, and the objective of the game is to destroy the opposing player's fleet. Galt's bench is all about trying to sink another team. Need a QB? He's got two top tier. One of Rex's other running back gets hurt? He's got you covered.

7. Make Hud Great Again - Operation - I'd be willing to bet that Hud could not finish a game of Operation today. Much like his(and Shains) annual disregard of the waiver wire, it would be shaky at best. As of this post Saquon, whos out for the year, is not only still on Hud's team but in his starting line up.

8. TEAM REPEAT - Checkers - Trades don't happen often in the league but when they do, man o man. I understand Julio is hurt(which happens annually), and you lost CMC but this is the most desperate I've seen Rex since this one time in the by the pool table at 519...nevermind.

9. Shains Annual $100 Donation - Pictionary - Shain's team name draws out exactly what it is. Five minute check of the waiver wire Tuesday or Wednesday while you poop to improve your team? No thanks from Shainline.

10. Ball So Hard University - Mouse Trap - The past few years Taco has let it be known that his strategy involves losing the first couple weeks to snag the bet guys off the waiver wire. This is year the mouse gets caught in his own trap.
