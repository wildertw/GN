2021 Week 4: Rainbow Woahd

I had this one queued up for a while. With no changing in the ranking last week, I chose to take the week off from the write up. I did find of stack of old write-ups that I printed. I am going to get them laminated along with the rules book. If it comes out halfway decent, I can get copies made for any of you that want some coffee table material. Anyways Mario Kart 64 items is the topic of the week. Sorry if this brings back any bad memories you have of Wario and me giggling while blazing past you on Toad’s Turnpike.

1.Lightning--Solution to the Markish Questio—Everyone knows you must be in the back of the pack to get lightning. Waivers drafted with the last pick and his lightning has struck gold. I told Waivers my topic of the week to which he replied “Wanna know the difference between Mario Kart and Sex? I always finish last in Mario Kart” …

2.Star--Witty, Griddy, and Ch – Galt’s team does not lack star power. But that is not what I want to talk about…I am not sure what is going on with this team name. What does the CH stand for? Does it stand for Carolina Hurricanes? With Cheese? Or is it Methylene? Switzerland? Chapel Hill? Or maybe it stands for Currently Horny? Cunt Hair? Is it for Clyde Edwards-Helaire the man commonly referred to as CEH? The same CH that has yet to crack his lineup. Galt I am onto your tactics…

3.Three Red Shells--Mike's Cheesefakes—In the middle of a rough stretch in which Shain is facing the other four-fifths top teams outside of himself over a four-week period. Taking down Galt two weeks ago before slipping up against Hud this past week. With Rex and Waivers to come, will Shain be able to shoot them down?

4.Boo--That ONE Time—In game Boo is used to steal an item(s) from another player. Hud has not stolen anything besides Ben Macdoo’s haircut (link if you want to see for yourself https://www.panthers.com/team/coaches-roster/ben-mcadoo ). Could the ghost of Hud make his second ever playoff appearance and first since 2014?

5.Three Green Shells--Throwing Bombs and BangingMoms – Rex was kind enough to gift me my first win on the season. It felt dirty after he had both of his running backs (who were also his 1st and 2nd round picks) go down. Javonte’s done for the year and JT is in a walking boot with the dreaded high ankle sprain. Rex is going to need some green shells for protection over the next few weeks.

6.Golden Mushroom--Ball So Hard University—Wood ate a golden mushroom once but that is neither here nor there. His best chance of continuous success in Mario Kart was getting multiple golden mushrooms in a row while mashing Z to no end. His only chance to make the playoffs is if every time King Henry gets the ball, he is mashing Z with a golden mushroom. FUN LEAGUE FACT OF THE WEEK: OVER THE PAST THREE MATCHUPS WOOD HAS SCORED: 99.2 ,97.5, & 99.5(1-2 two losses by a combined 5.7points). PERSISTENCE AND CONSISTENCY IS THE KEY.

7.Blue Shell--Leather Cheerios—Blue shell takes out first.

8.Banana Bunch--A center for Ants—I do not foresee Ian dancing come playoff time but there are more than a few teams that he could slip up. Ian almost got the fun league fact of the week since him nor Wood have spent a dollar of their FAAB. That is a bold strategy Cotton, lets see how it works out for them.

9.Fake Question Box--Baseball Season—Unfortunately for Potter none of the Questionable marks by his players are not fake. There are better days ahead for team Potter once(if) they get healthy.

10.One Banana--Team Rectal Discharge—I promise I will not do this all year but this one was too easy.
