Super Villains

Week 11: Super Evil

It is finally here boys, last week of the regular season. To date I can not think of a more interesting week 12 in league history. The top 3 are basically set; Potter will be the one seed, while Hud and I will face off in round 1. This is where it starts to get fun, there are four teams with playoff hopes and guess who the fuck they're playing this week?! Each Other. Chris Berman would shit his pants if something like this happened in the NFL. Waivers vs Rex, Clod vs Galt. Now if anyone wants to change the rules for the final week and we go gladiator style fight to the death or maybe an old school WWF cage fight we can take it to a vote. Also, and most importantly, we have a battle to end for the Maddy. Ian, Taco, and Shain all still have a shot to call her theirs. And who do bottle of barrel play this week? The top three in the league. Ouch. Super division worked out this year as the best team will make the playoffs.

This week is a precursor to next week. This week I am going to compare everyone to super villains, next will be super heros. I wish each of you the best of luck, haha actually who I am kidding? Fuck all of you this is war.

The Joker - Potter - Lovers of chaos, full of surprises, ability to tell a story second only to Morgan Freeman, inability to smile due to a subpar jawline and awful gruesome jokes. In all aspects of life and fantasy. If the Joker ever met Pot(before he killed him) he would without question ask him "Why so serious?" Potter has all but locked up the number one seed in the playoffs. Potter's team is loaded with henchmen, and the Joker always seemed to have the best and also the dumbest. Gronk has regained his he-man form, Demarco and Arian have been running wild. Then you have Cutler, whoever Potter puts in the flex, and Arian Foster's groin. Will the Dark Knight rise or will evil prevail?

Loki - Hud - Evil brother of Thor. Resentful to everyone in fantasy football due to ineffectiveness on the fantasy front as Loki is resentful to Thor for not being adequate to take over the thrown. Loki has only become recently relevant on the big stage, as Hud has this year. On top of all that, Loki is a cocky fucker and well Hud isn't shy. Hud's power from Forte, Bell, Brees, and Sanders has led him this far as the only thing this powerful in Hud's life is left hand. Will Thor smash his mighty hammer down on Loki or will Loki finally get his respect that he only thinks he deserves?

Magneto - Trey - Keeper of all the evil X-men this was relatively easy. Magneto and myself are the babysitters of our respected evil groups. Our high quality shaping abilities always help us craft a quality team. I could be the root of all evil and sit everyone on my team so Ian wins to make things that much more interesting. hmm...

Juggernaut - Rex - If someone came up to me and said that a league member walked up to them, then drunkly mumble " I'm the Juggernaut bitch!". I would buy the poor man a drink and apologize for what Rex had done. From screaming at 3a.m. that Lewis street was "his street"(lol) to making unnumbered amount of people bow to him. Juggernaut and Rex are big bullies plain in simple. While his fantasy team is in comparison to Juggernaut he will face the fantasy team of our real life Miguel "Das Kraken" Waivers. Spider Y 2 Banana did not have shit on hand the ball to Waivers in elementary school football. Jamaal, Jordy, and Juggernaut shall lead Rex to the hopeful promiseland, as Rex has a substantial point lead but with a week to play anything is possible.

The Penguin - Clod - Clod and Danny DeVito could be twins.

Bane - Waivers - No one cared about Bane until he put on the mask. No one really gave a shit about Waivers' team until week 12 which he could make the playoffs. From the slums of the league Waivers team has risen as only Waivers could. If I was any of the other three niggas left fighting for a playoff spot, I would check out going to a witch doctor, maybe get a voodoo doll. You will all need a superior power to break the Waiversing that he taken yall on the ride for. Bane is here to fuck shit up, one of yall needs to be batman.

Green Goblin- Galt - Spiderman's arch nemesis, cocky, quick, arrogant, and always quick to the draw. Galt's team has given him a ride on the Goblin's hoverboard all year long. Galt is sitting with the fewest points scored among the final four, making his chances as about as good as me beating him in golf. Hey, even a blind squirrel finds a nut every now and then. Other than the next supervillain on this list, I believe Galt would make a near perfect Green Goblin. Intelligent but sometimes has the tendency to flirt with the is he that smart or is he that crazy? Hopefully for Galt his hoverboard leads him to victory and not in his stomach.

The Riddler - Shain - Hickory dickory dock. Why does Shain's team suck so much cock? Did he not know there was a training block. Was the spot on his jock and a little pillow talk for Maddy's pink sock? Now that's a riddle. As you will see in this week's caption contest Shain strikingly resembles Jim Carrey as the Riddler. Even there high pitched evil laugh is the exact same. Shain's team has been a riddle all season, as have much of the decisions that he has made. Shain, I think you are the only one in the group that could pull off the tight green suit with ?s. Shain took down Taco last week for his coveted 4th win. Barring another user error Shain should be Marlowe free despite taking on Potter this week.

Catwoman - Wood - Taco has a complex love/hate relationship with his team as she does with Batman. Wood thought he had made quite a few steals taking Peyton, Brandon Marshall, and Julius Thomas he quickly found out he was wrong. I need you more than ever right now. Think about Goat. She will have to feast her poor sweet innocent little eyes on that horrendous thing. She does not need a picture of another animal in this house. Maybe next year you will actually stay in your room during the draft.

Poison Ivy - Ian - One time I had a friend who got so fucked up he thought the swamp donkey was Poison Ivy's sexy self. Wasn't Ian. In Ian's first year in the league in looks likely that Maddy will become his. He still has a fighting chance but just as Poison Ivy mesmerized Batman and Robin, I believe this fight is over.
