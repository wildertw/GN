Opening pitch

Are you a grown man who spends four months a year staring at your phone, convincing yourself that this Sunday will finally be different? Have you blown hundreds of dollars on fantasy football, ignored your responsibilities, and still somehow finished ninth?

We’ve selected ten genuine As Seen on TV products and paired them with ten men who are somehow even less reliable than the shit being advertised.

How the odds were calculated
  • Lock in Weeks 1–8: each team’s actual wins and points, plus its mean and sample standard deviation of its eight actual scores. Nothing from prior seasons, projections or lineups is used.
  • Simulate Weeks 9–13 from the real schedule: each team draws one normal score per week from its own mean and SD (floored at zero, rounded to one decimal), and that score decides the matchup and adds to points for.
  • Rank by record, then season points, with a seeded random draw for exact ties. Top four make the playoffs, ninth and tenth make the Caddy Bowl. Seed 20261105, NumPy PCG64.

Eight games is a small sample, and the model ignores injuries, byes, waiver moves and lineup mistakes. There is no bye adjustment. These are odds of a top-four finish, not championship odds.
