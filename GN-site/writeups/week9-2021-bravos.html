2021 · Week 9

Bravos

We are through 8 weeks of the fantasy season. Every year up to this point would be the ⅔ point of the season but the NFL added a game. Which will have MAJOR fantasy implications. Some teams would be dead in the water without the extra game. Praise Goodell! Week 8 was brutal. Injuries, backup quarterbacks, Potter cried, Wood didn’t eat candy, waiver wire gems, Rex didn’t wear cologne, Monday night miracles. It had it all. Peak football season. What better way to celebrate peak football season than comparing teams to the Braves. A team that plays in the 4th biggest sports league in America. We’re all Braves fans tonight…

  1. Hank Aaron - Chasen’ A Dream - Some may argue Hammerin Galt does not deserve the top spot. After all, Clod does sit atop the league in wins but more on that later. Despite the upset loss last week to last place Wood, Galt’s team is a force. Would a quick QB change from Hurts to Stafford given him the win last week? I am too lazy to check. Galt likely does not deserve ranking this being a rumored commie/anti-american.
  2. Chipper Jones - Roy johnson in dis bish - The greatest third baseman of all time. Has Shain ever been the best at anything in fantasy? Subtle remarks and unexpected burns? Probably not the best but he gives it a good try. Chipper was the greatest switch hitter of all time. Shain’s team can hit you from both sides of the plate. He fought injuries through his career, Shain’s team has caught the injury bug as bad as any of us. Can Shain replicate Chipper and continue to rake while being held together by rubber band and chewing gum?
  3. Greg Maddux - We better win the ship - The Mad Dog. I have thought about how Potter resembles the Mad Dog but it is normally the look on his face between 12:30-4am for a night on the town. I know Potter wanted Chipper and I even gave him an extra week to try and catch the top to get there. He’ll accept this. Depending on the result of the game tonight he may not even read this. Can Potter paint the corners to make the big dance? His remaining schedule is looking like the toughest among the playoff contenders with only one game against a team with a losing record.
  4. Tom Glavine - Discount double check - I have a bold prediction that Ian will rename his Jonathin the in my Taylors. Glavine was dubbed the crafty lefty. Ian has a way of crafting his teams. No, it's not to draft Mike Evans and Tyreek Hill every year. There is the same consistency Glavine used to have on the mound.
  5. John Smoltz - I’ll be at the bar - Clod sits atop the league currently so why is he 5th in the WPI you ask. King Henry is down for the count. I have witnessed Clod be up and then be down and back up and way back down in the blink of an eye more times than I can count. I am challenging Clod’s team to reinvent themselves in a way that Smoltzy did becoming a closer.
  6. Freddy Freeman - The Goatfather - Both unhateable. Love to hug. Both have a championship on the horizon this year.
  7. Fred McGriff - Mikedale Stoopkids - The Crimedog. Tommy Emanski would be devastated by Waivers lack of fundamentals. Starting an injured Quarterback is giving up and the Crimedog would never give up. It doesn’t feel right giving Waivers such a cool nickname. He is incapable of ever living up to the name.
  8. Dan Uggla - Ball So Hard University - Dan Uggla knew how to do two things on the diamond, hit bombs and strike out. Oddly that seems to be the case with Woods fantasy career. Striking out way more times.
  9. Deion Sanders - The Replacements - Deion played two sports. Rex has only talked about HIS league lately claiming they play for more money or something so it's more important to him. I heard through the grapevine there isn’t even a group chat in that league. And I am positive there are no league notes. I am fine playing for whatever amount the league is comfortable playing for and will bet you separately every year any amount that I will better you. Name your price. Money ain’t that only motive for me here.
  10. John Rocker - Make hud great again - For those that do not know, Rocker was known for his questionable statements. If you have ever spent more than 30 minutes drinking with Hud in public you know where I am going with this. It’s not that we aren’t thinking about it too, it just comes out of Hud’s mouth. Love or hate him for it.
