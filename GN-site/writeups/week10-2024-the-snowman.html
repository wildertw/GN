2024 · Week 10

The Snowman

Once again it's on, I'm back in the motherfuckin’ booth. 10 weeks in and Jeezy may be the only answer. The advanced Treyalytics (courtesy of www.theffhud.com (I just copy and paste)) have Rex locked into the playoffs at 100% with a 70% chance of holding the 1 seed followed by Potter with a 96% chance to make the playoffs and a 27% chance at the one seed. Has this become a two man race with three weeks to go? Spoiler alert: they are 1-2 in the power rankings. Oh yeah, they do play each other next week. I am going to try and highlight every team's best and worst draft pick so far and maybe best FAAB picks. Three weeks to go, chaos is in the air, and only the strongest survive. Let’s see who’s thriving, who’s barely holding on, and who’s on life support. Lets get it

  1. Potter - Supafreak/Trapstar - I couldn't pick between the two. Derrick The King Henry really is a Supafreak. His decision to keep the King is his best “draft pick” but for the sake of it being a draft pick we will go with Kamara who is currently second in RB scoring behind Henry. That’s one Supafreaky backfield. His worst pick is probably Keenan Allen which did not come till round 9. Potter's team has been on their grind scoring in the top three in four out of the past five weeks and seven out of the ten weeks of the season. Potter thinks he's got the league on lock and big scores are in store and he likes it.
  2. Rex - I luv it - As stated earlier, Rex has all but locked up a playoff spot and he loves yeah, Rex is on a roll, Playoff vibes hittin’ hard, got that ultimate goal. I should have made Rex “Hustlerz ambition” after the way he has hustled this year. We will start with the ugly, he could have kept Kyren or AJB but was star struck Marvin Harrison Jr., he has also lost two(at the time) bonafide WR1s to season injuries but after all those woes he still has a playoff spot on lock with 3 weeks to go. His wheel and deal to get London has been noted enough, and Cade Otten looks to be one of the best waiver pickups of the year.
  3. Ian - Put On - After losing two of his last three, Ian needs his team to do what they have done time and time again. Put on for his team. I feel like Mahomes just got the ball with 3 minutes to go and the games tied. Ian currently sits with 54% chance of making the playoffs. Coach once again had a solid draft even without his keepers. Kittle and Aaron Jones lead the pack for best “value picks”. I have to mention Kenneth Walker. Downs and Tracy have a couple of the better Waiver moves of the year. Coach will have his team putting on as they do when the weather turns cold.
  4. Trey - Soul Survivor - What seemed like Trey was a shoo-in three weeks ago, he is now just hoping to survive till playoff time.
  5. Clod- And then what - Clod has taken the punches this year and has excelled? Clod currently sits with a 35% chance to make the playoffs. Clod may have the hardest remaining schedule in the league with Waivers and a healthy CMC, Hud( more on him below), and league leading scorer Potter looming in the next three weeks. Will he defy the odds and sneak in?
  6. Hud - Lose my mind - Since ending his league leading losing streak, Hud has been the weekly highest scorer three weeks in a row. That has only been done three times in league history (Rex 2011, Shain 2014). Hud has to be losing his mind as he could have the best team in the league and 0.96% chance of making the playoffs. Everyone ready to vote on the new rule? Hud’s working of the waiver wire has been unreal, scoring Baker, Mooney, and Sutton all for less than $8. Meanwhile Trey spent $8 on a D/ST this week…
  7. Galt - Leave you alone - As promised Galt, I will leave you alone about the Zack Moss trade. In fact I will do you a favor and give you some advice. You can safely cut your losses and drop Moss as he has been ruled out for the rest of the regular season unless you are holding him for morale reasons. I have gotten my laugh out of looking at him still on your team for long enough. It is time for you to leave him alone. Galt’s picks in round 12 of Chubba and Jayden in round 9 were two of the better picks of the draft. His re-add of Calvin Ridley ($3) is looking like his best waiver wire move thus far. Losing four out of his last five all but killed his playoff chances and is now looking to play spoiler with only a 5% chance of making the playoffs or will he tank for Ashton Jeanty?
  8. Waivers - Seen it all - Despite his current power ranking, Waivers is currently sitting with a 36% chance to make the playoffs and after the season he’s had, you could say he has “seen it all”. Two losses by three points combined, CMC’s Achilleses, Jamo suspended, now Cooper Rush throwing to CD when will it end? His team does appear to be getting healthy and rounding into form at the right time, putting himself in a prime spot to Waivers his way into the yoffs. Waiver’s wire picks have been absent for most of the year with his largest bid being $21 on Jordan Addison(who along with Courland Sutton have been league sluts being on 4 different teams so far this year).
  9. Wood - Bottom of the Map - After looking like they had turned things around, Wood finds himself in familiar territory at the bottom of the league. Losing three in a row and now facing the possibility of being Caddy. Will Wood be able to avoid the daunted last place as he has every year since 2009? Outside of his keepers and Lamar it is hard to find a “good” draft pick by Wood. Here's his picks round 5-8 in order: DJ Moore, Zamir White, Amari Cooper, Jaylen Warren. Gross. Mike Evans is due back in week 12. Will that help? Wood currently ranks last in scoring from the following positions: WR, TE, and D/ST. This week's matchup has MAJOR caddy bowl implications as Wood and Shain have faltered while Hud has caught fire.
  10. Shain - RIP - I don’t know where to start with Shain’s team. He has been the lowest weekly scorer three weeks in a row and a bottom three scorer the past six weeks. Losing six in a row. Clinging to life. Injuries have without question played their part. A loss this week, and it’s lights out, Caddy cap in hand. RIP.
