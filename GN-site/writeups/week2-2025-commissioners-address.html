2025 · Week 2

Commissioner’s Address

Let me start with this and I want this to be the first, last, and only time I address it. This is a FANTASY football league. That means jokes, jabs, gabs, pranks, ball-busting, and every other form of degeneracy a boy can dream up. If you have some kind of a personal beef with someone outside of this league, keep that shit out of here.

Yes, things get heated. Yes, words sometimes slip that weren’t meant to land the way they do. But this year it’s become more of a distraction than ever. Gentlemen, we are in our mid-to-late thirties. Let’s fucking act like it.

I’ll handle the league matters, and that’s it. This isn’t Communist China.

Alas, I wasn’t planning on writing this week, but as the great commissioner that I am, here I am, carrying the weight of this league on my broad, fantasy-god shoulders. Let’s dive into the noteworthy stats I whiffed on last week.

1a) Hud

We are officially in uncharted waters. This is only the third time Hud has started 2-0 (2009 & 2021). After setting the league record with 11 straight losses, he’s flipped the script with a personal-best eight straight wins.

Now, some will say Hud has played the weakest schedule. They’d be right. But as Coach Boone said: “It doesn’t matter if it’s by an inch or a mile, a win is a win.” Winnings winning.

Omarian Hampton, though… possibly the bust of the draft? Hud’s season probably hinges on whether Omarian wakes up, which is also why Hud currently sits at 1a, not 1 outright.

1b) Cody

Did not see this one coming. On draft night, I called Clod’s squad boom or bust. I’ve since changed my stance: it’s chaos wrapped in destiny.

After nearly getting fleeced by Potter in the offseason, the fantasy gods smiled down and gifted him a Baker Mayfield game-winning drive against Potter’s Texans D/ST, giving Cody his second career sub-1 point win… both against Potter. (2016 was the first.)

Cody has finished 4th place six times (nobody else has more than twice), and all signs point to him living in that cursed spot again.

3) Ian

And just like that, Coach is back. James Cook (RB1 overall) is frying defenses like it’s his side hustle, and if this continues, we might as well all pack it in.

E holds a league-best 16-2 power record. Translation: if he had anyone else’s schedule besides mine and Potter’s, he’d be undefeated. Good coaching > good luck.

4) Potter

The league’s chaos agent. Potter just wants to watch the world burn, and I despise him for it.

Shockingly, no whining after his MNF heartbreak. Growth? Doubtful. If he misses the playoffs, we’ll never escape the “Bowers-CMC-Kamara” soap opera he scripted for himself.

Also: how the hell do you always get Brandon Aubrey? Potter is just 88.9 points away from joining me in the 22K Club.

5) Trey

Apparently Brian Thomas Jr. spent his offseason auditioning for the Broadway production of The Phantom of the Pussy. And it’s going to cost me my playoff hopes. Sigh.

6) Lee

Lee is more of a 5b than a 6, but we’re not making subcategories a thing here.

The question on everyone’s mind: how bad is Jayden’s knee? Lee skipped over every QB on waivers, so either he knows something we don’t, or he’s building his team around sheer vibes.

Tyler Warren = stud. Galt faces his career personal toughest matchup this week in T-Wood, where he’s 6-12 lifetime.

7) Rex

This feels low, his team name is already the winner of the year. If you don’t get the reference, friend of the league and future NYT best selling romance novel king, Lee Shaver, just published his first masterpiece. Here is a link with one of my favorite quotes from the book: “ Vomit mouth and Everything “.

Slow start for Rex, but it won’t last. Unfortunately, he draws Waivers this week, against whom he holds a personal-worst 38% win rate. Yikes.

8) Waivers

Anyone from 4–8 could swap places right now. The top three have pulled away, and Waivers’ roster consistency won’t keep him this low forever. Patience.

9) Shain

I’m sorry, brother. Truly. But here we are.

Shain has now tied Hud’s infamous record from last year with ELEVEN straight losses. Just a year ago, he was 3-1 and staring down his hopes of his first five-game win streak. How quickly the tides turn.

I fully expect Shain to beat me this week handily and make me eat every word I’ve just typed.

10) Wood

Remember when Wood was relevant? 2017–2019: 2nd, 1st, 3rd. Since then: 8th, 7th, 5th, 9th, 9th. Statistically, one of if not the worst five year runs in league history and somehow stayed away from last place.

Now, with Toe Burrow out, things look even bleaker. Maybe Indiana Jones can rescue this sinking ship. Or maybe call Waivers about that CMC-for-Bowers rumor? Just a thought.

Not that Wood will read this anyway. But hey, it’s on record.
