Gatorade

Week 2: Back in my day we called it Gater Ate

What a week 2…trying to remain positive…We had two comeback games come down to the Monday night double header. Hud beat demolished Potter for the second time in league history and first since 2017. There are currently four teams at (2-0). League history shows us this means nothing. Last year there was four teams to start (2-0) yet only one made the playoffs with eventual champion Ian starting the year (0-3) and failing to crack 100 points in a matchup before reeling off 10 straight victories. The year before three teams starting (2-0) with all three making the playoffs. Year before (2019) four teams started (2-0), three made the play offs. 2018 had two teams start (2-0) neither made the playoffs. 2017, three team with only one making the big dance at the end. I am not going back any further out of laziness. The point is do not give up unless your name rhymes with Snarcus Botter.

Week 2s power rankings will be Gatorade flavors. Gatorade flavors won a hard-fought battle with Adam Sandler movies. I had Waivers as “The Wedding Singer” with the punchline of the joke being “Did he even have a wedding singer?!.” Galt was going to be Happy Gilmore for his unconventional ways. Potter had “Little Nicky” for their matching haircuts and the probable chance he is a spawn of Satan. He scored exactly 66.6 last week, need I say more.

1. Lemon Lime- “Mark Uses Jaw Line Supplement”—Now that is fantasy football name Waivers. You could slightly tweak it but none the less it is a top tier name to match a top tier team. If someone asked me today what the best Gatorade flavor is, I am going with the classic. If I get asked “who has the best fantasy team?” this week, I am going with Waivers. Topping 140 points weeks in a row. His wide receiver core that is so scary I do not even look at it. Plus, Josh Allen. There may be a hiccup here and there but its going to take a lot for Waivers lemons to sour this year. FUN LEAGUE FACT OF THE WEEK: WEEK 2 2021 WAIVERS ALSO SCORED EXACTLY 66.6. Are these two demons from the underworld?

2. Glacier Freeze— “Mike’s Cheesefakes”—I did not think anyone would top Waiver’s new team name but then Shain had to fuck around and get all silly stupid funny creative. It’s a++. There is much debate as to what Gatorade belongs in this spot (it’s second and by far the best blue Gatorade), there is little doubt that it is a top-class flavor. Shain has a top-class team and looks to make the playoffs for the first time since 2016. Shain texted me on Monday “Might not even bench Diggs on his bye” and I do not blame him.

3. Riptide Rush— “Witty and Griddy”—Despite a lackluster Monday performance from first overall pick Justin Jefferson, Galt edged out Trey’s massive comeback attempt with a stout performance from the Bills D. Their performance earned Lee Riptide Rush for namesake alone. Their twenty points were almost a third of Potters total points scored this week. Week 3 will feature a heavyweight match between unbeaten Shain & Galt. Ding. Ding.

4. Orange—“Throwing Bombs and banging moms”—Before switching topics, Rex’s Adam Sandler movie was “Big Daddy” with all thanks being to Big Dick Daddy Hurts willing his team to victory on Monday night. I did not check the live odds but as a betting man I’d say wood was over 90% projected to win this game. I have thrown up orange Gatorade enough that its undrinkable at this point despite being in my top 2 at one time. If Rex sees too many single digit games out of his running back, he will experience the same nausea.

5. Fierce Grape—"That one TIME”—Hud did it. Defeating Potter for the second time in seventeen attempts. Now he only must win 15 in a row to get back to .500 against potter. That could happen by 2030. Fierce Grape gets slept on a lot and I think a lot of us are sleeping on Hud’s team. His WR trio rivals Waivers trio for best in the league and he has CMAC. Hud has not made the playoffs since 2014(his only appearance). Will this finally be the year he breaks through?

6. Strawberry—"Ball So Hard Women’s College”—Not to be confused with Strawberry-Kiwi, this could be the best flavor when it all comes down to it. Do I feel the same way about Woods fantasy team being one of the best? In short, no. I do not.

7. Cool Blue-“A center for ants”—Anyone that tells you Cool Blue is the best blue flavored Gatorade deserves to have their tongue removed on the spot. It does have a cool name unlike ^^^^^^^. It’s hard to doubt the back-to-back champ. Ians going need Big Blue to keep feeding Saquan if he wants to have a chance this year.

8. Rain Berry-“Leather Cheerios”—Did they stop making this flavor? I do not want to talk about my team, just want an answer.

9. Cucumber-Lime—“Chad Powers”—Clod chugging down Cucumber-Lime. It is funnier if you think about it as not a Gatorade flavor.

10. All G2 Flavors—"2023 my year FO SHO”—The only thing FO SHO is that G2 is trash compared to normally Gatorade. I will fight that forever. Diet Gatorade sucks ass. I have made some subtle jabs at Potter throughout this writeup. Do I want to stop here? No but I am going to.
