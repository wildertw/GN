Week 7: Best Toons of the 90s

I dropped the ball. My last write-up was my worst of the season; I had just suffered my second loss and I felt like dog shit. That's my excuse and I'm sticking to it. This week I'm back, feeling better than ever. I am going to rank and compare all teams to my personal favorite(and least favorite) 90s cartoons. Good luck to all this week.

1. Power Puff Girls - Potter - I called Potter in an attempt to ask him which cartoon he wanted to be. Upon answering the phone he began to whine. Women whine, potters a woman, and so are the Power Puff Girls. Just like a pop tart Potter and his team is hard on the outside. He thinks his combination of sugar, spice, and everything nice had created an unbeatable animal but oh was he wrong(as he found out last). Blossom(Demarco) the self proclaimed leader has the unique power of freezing things with her brain. My prediction is by the end of the year she freezes Potter's brain once he hears "And Demarco is down and it does not look good". Bubbles(Arian)the cute and sensitive one can communicate with animals. Potter's dog will hear something that says "eat Mark's phone and computer" minutes before kickoff causing him several miscues. Buttercup(Antonio) the toughest on of the group but has no super power. He will stick around but begin to show he is not

great.

2. Captain Planet - Trey - Everyone sing it with me "Captain Trey he's our hero, going to take Potter down to zero, he's our powers magnified, And he's fighting on the league's side". EARTH(Marshawn)! WIND(Ellington)! FIRE(Dez)! WATER(Luck)! HEART(Miller/Wallace)! I am hear to save the league with my teams powers combined.

3. Dexter's Lab - Hud - Where is Hud? He's not hiding, he has been in his lab crafting together a solid fantasy football team. He is the smartest boy we have ever seen, have you ever seen bee bee blow up his team to smithereens. Hud will need Forte and Bell to keep this up for the remainder of the year, for it they do not it will be another destroyed super genius team of Hud's that gets destroyed.

4. Johnny Bravo - Clod - Hmm nah! Hey little lady! Quick question, who else in our league brings a comb with out and about? Clods the only one? Thought so. Clod once called the police because there was a "handsome man" in his mirror only to realize he was talking about himself. Clod's team on the other hand should spend plenty of time looking themselves in the mirror as they look studly. The 3 headed monster of Gio, Shady, and Hillman could be Johnny's comb, Russel could be his cologne and Julio could be his shades. If Johnny can keep his look together for the year, he could hit a homer.

5. Pinky and the Brain - Galt - Every night Galt opens up his computer and says "What are going to do tonight Pinky" which Galt snickers back "The same thing we do every night, try and take over the league". Over the past few weeks Galt's team has become hampered by injuries from Ball to Graham to Doug Martin. Although with all becoming healthy. What will Galt do? Will he attempt to take over the league? Galt has fought through his issues to have a winning record at 4-3 but with tilts against the three highest scoring teams and a 5-2 JHud he will need his injured players to come back and make an immediate impact.

6. Ed, Edd, n Eddy - Rex - Ed, Edd, and Eddy could describe the stages of Rex from sober to drunk. Eddy(Sober Rex) Labeled a megalomaniac by his report card, Eddy loves being the center of attention as the unofficial leader. Though he pretends to know it all, his knowledge is often suspect or incomplete. Edd(4 Beers Rex) Edd is really smart, really quiet and unnaturally polite. Ed(Blackout Rex) He's easily talked into whatever hare-brained scheme Eddy has dreamed up, and his uncanny physical strength often comes in handy. Rex has caught his fair share of bad luck this season like that time in third grade they told Rex his best friend was a 2x4 named "Plank". Which Ed will lead Rex's team the rest of the way?

7. Doug - Ian - Ian is the new guy in the league as Doug as the new guy in school. Everyone loves Lambchop, everyone loves Marley(and Esse). But as we have learned by reading Ian's journal there is a big bully in the league, Potter(Roger). Ian has ran into some predicaments in his first season of the league. Calvin Johnson and Reggie Wayne have both gone down. Also Doug had a better chance of getting the Beats to play at his birthday party than Ian has a chance of have a decent running back core. Luckily for Ian he has found his Patty Mayonnaise in Demariyus Thomas as E and Doug have wet mayonnaise dreams every night to their respective Pattys.

8. Hey Arnold - Wood - I am not really sure who looks funnier in a hat, Taco or Arnold? They're the exact opposite of one another, Arnold's football shaped head is way too small for his hat, and Wood's pea shaped head. Wood's friends also resemble Arnolds Grandpa(Me), Gerald(Potter), Helga Pataki(Casey), Harold(Rex), Stinky(Clod), Robert "Big Bob" Pataki(Waiver's). Wood's team has taken a turn for the worst. After starting 2-0 it has been five weeks since Wood has been in the win colum. Wood really needs to get his production from his first three draft picks. I am not sure what would be more embarrassing, him finding Casey's shrine similar to Helga shrine of Arnold's hair, gum, and used q-tips or him being the reason I have to look at Maddy day and night.

9. Ren and Stimpy - Waivers - I know, before you claim it, that this is the best cartoon of the 90s. Waiver's team started more like Ren, the emotionally unstable chihuahua. Losing five out of his first six matchups had Waivers about to lose his mind. Then came along Stimpy the dim-witted happy go LUCKY cat. Waivers has found some luck along the waiver wire in Brady and Forsett. Due to get back Ryan Matthews and AJ Green to go along with getting Mark Ingram back. Will this have Waivers doing the happy dance at the end of the year or the fappy dance to Maddy?

10. Spongebob - Shain - Are you ready kids? I can't hear you! Are you ready? Who lives in a pineapple at the bottom of the league? Shainline Squareapants! Whose imporant Marlowe heiress can we all see? Shainline Squarpants! If nautical nonsense is something you wish, Shainline Squarepants! Then put Maddy on the wall and cry like bitch! Shainline Squarepants! Shaineline Squarepants! Shaiiinnnnnnnnnneeeelllllllline Squarepants!
