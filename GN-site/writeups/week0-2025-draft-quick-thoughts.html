2025 · Week 0

Draft Quick Thoughts

Shain - Nabbing Jeanty with the first pick was a no brainer. Generational running backs don’t come around often in fantasy. Nico, AJ Brown, and Sutton round out a solid receiving core. ETN in the 9th round. I really like what Shain did and think he will be in playoff contention pending his ability to navigate his team throughout the year.

Wood - Like our beloved Panthers, his whole season will ride on the back of Tet McMillan(and arm of Bryce Young?). Burrow, Gibbs, and Bowers could all finish tops their respective positions and it would shock no one. Will that be enough to get over his league leading five year playoff drought hump?

Waivers - This team is so good, Mike could trade away CMC for Brock Bowers and be near unstoppable if it weren’t for Potter’s super team with CMC. After years of flirting with the skinhead haircut, Waivers may be the whitest team in league history ever assembled with CMC, Ladd McConkey, Ricky Pearsall, Sam Laporta, and Trevor Lawrence. He told me before the draft he was “looking for gym rats, real lunch pale guys, ya know”, and it looks like he got what he was aiming for. All jokes aside, if Lawrence can live up to his pedigree, and you know who stays healthy he could be on the way to his second* title.

Ian - After his first time missing the playoffs in four years, we all know the old ball coach had plenty of time to get his team back to basics of their championship ways. I could hear E giggling to himself all the way over here when he took Tyreek Hill. What shocked me is when he passed on Mike Evans. They say the best coaches know when it’s over.

Cody - I am not sure what to think of Clod’s team. There’s a ton of risk but with that risk comes the reward. If it all clicks this team blows the rest out of the water, if it doesn’t Clod drafted the three rookie running backs and Travis Hunter leaving him a running back keeper he so badly yearned for.

Hud - The most surprising thing that happened early on the draft was Omarion Hampton falling to Hud at pick 25. Pair him with Chase and St Brown and Hud could have three picks in the top 15 again this year. Even though I think it was a slight reach, Swift has the chance to be a high end RB2 if the Bears offense takes the step everyone(not me) thinks that it will. The talent is there for this team to have championship aspirations. The coach….

Lee - I mock drafted what I thought everyone in this league would do in the draft, but kept coming up with one issue. How does one think like a Galt while drafting? I got scared then excited then cried. In a way that is how many of Galt’s fantasy seasons have gone. I am fan of how his team came together. Chase Brown has a chance to be a monster that he wanted Zach Moss to be. This is the only league I do not have Jayden Daniels on my team if that tells you how high I personally(along with JOC) am on him but I was happy to settle for Josh Allen. Terry to go with Jayden is icing on the cake. Tyler Warren should be a weapon. If Lee wins this year the question we will all ask is how did we let Breece Hall fall this far.

Trey - I don’t think anyone will read mine but having three guys that could finish tops at their position in Saquon, Josh Allen, and Kittle gives every reason to be excited about this team. Hot take, Davante Adams leads the league in TDs and our team to the fantasy title. Thanks to everyone that let him fall to round 6. Rashee Rice and BTJ will give people nightmares come playoff time. Hopefully it's not me.

Rex - I was surprised Rex didn’t keep Jacobs but to each their own. Drake London is no slouch and could easily finish as a top three WR. Mike Evans in round six is crazy value(wtf happened in round 6?). Lamar Jackson is Lamar Jackson. I thought Rex and I would be battling back and forth all night on picks but that's not how the cookie crumbled. Rex looks poised to make another playoff run extending his league leading record of playoff appearances to 11.

Potter - The champion, I apologize. I had ordered you a cameo congratulating you on your accomplishment but it has not come through yet. Potter’s team looks eerily similar to last year. It worked for him last year, why can’t it work this year? Anyways that’s enough about Potter winning and I am tired of writing nice things about any of you.
