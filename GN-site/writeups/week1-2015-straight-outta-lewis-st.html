2015 · Week 1

Straight Outta Lewis St

Straight Outta Lewis St

The first week has come and gone. As exciting as it was just mere days ago, unfortunately I had forgot how disappointing it can be. While I had enjoyed pretending to be friends with everyone(except Clod) for the past 36 weeks, it is that time of year I get to viciously hate on all of you. What better way than comparing everyone to gangsta rappers of the NWA days. Notice I said comparing, not ranking. These are in no order of who I think are the best rappers of their day and I know I left several rappers out. I tried to stick to NWA and a little bit of Death Row records, No Pac, Biggie, Wu-Tang(except one that just made too much since), or rappers of the 2000s: 50, Eminem, Nas, Jay-Z, ect.

You are now about to witness the strength of fantasy shit talking.

Tim Dog - Galt - Who? Originally I had Galt slated as another rapper, all Galt ever does is attempt to diss others in the league. Tim Dog was known for his song "Fuck Compton" and Eazy-E never thought it was necessary to diss him back. See the comparison? Galt's team did perform extremely well in the first week and my own expectations for his team have grown from what they were. Brady, Gronk, ABrown, and some how Carlos Hyde boomed this past week. My bold prediction will be that two of those four have scored their highest single week point total for the year. As dominate as the team looks on paper now, there still is holes in Galt's team; I could be wrong but let's be real how often is that? Galt I have already blocked your number so don't even bother texting me this week.

Ice Cube - Waivers - Straight outta Lewis St., Waivers is a crazy mother fucker with a bad attitude. Always looking to start a feud; Fuck the commissioner, that's how Waivers treats me. Now you're getting done without vaseline, damn it feels good for all the people to see. Yesterday was a good day for Waivers as Julio Jones beat on Potter worse than the boys from Office Space beat the fax machine. Waivers needs to check his self, his team is not as good as it seems.

Snoop Dogg - Ian - With so much drama in the L-E-W-I-S, it's kinda hard being Big E, but somehow, someway, he keeps looking at Maddy's funky ass every single day. Rollin down the street smokin indo, with his mind on the Maddie and the Maddie on his mind. Ian must have smoked some OG Grandaddy Purp Roller Coaster Bubble Gum Colombian Bam Bam kush before the draft by taking two quarterbacks as early as he did but through one week it has worked out for him. A-aron, Forte, and Shady make for a solid core that E is hoping will lead him to promise land of no more Maddie.

MC Ren - Hud - The forgotten member of NWA and the forgotten member of the league. Riddle me this, can anyone name an MC Ren song before the last time you saw Hud? Every year, somehow some way, I end up playing Hud and he beats me on some bullshit. You are a lucky sack of shit.

Eazy-E - Taco - A big part of me wanted to make Wood Dre but Wood is second most likely in the league to die of AIDs(*high-heel clink, high-heel clink,TAAAAAYLOORRR*). I heard it on a nightly basis. He is straight outta Lewis St. He cruised down the straight in his 94, talking the trash if you popped his car with a football, don't quote him boy, he don't remember shit. He was without question the greediest of all originals. His team, like him, currently has AIDs. They have all signs of dying a slow death, no Dez, no real RB1or2. I feel like we sat in this position last year that I almost have to pull for him to win so I do not feel the wrath of the Maddie.

Flava Flav - Shain - YEAHHHH BOBO!!! The most outlandish dresser of all league members. Outspoken in his own way, thinking they are greater than they are, I love this comparison. Flava Flav was an energy guy and that is the same as Shain. Just like Flava has made several terrible life decisions, Shain makes terrible league decisions on a regular basis. Going to wedding before setting his line up, drafting during some kind of cook-out, ect. When you look at Shain's team, it does not look that bad on paper but when you LOOK at it, it makes you cringe and wonder. I could eat my words but Maddie party in the Queen City could be epic.

Jerry Keller - Potter - Now, some of you may not even know who Jerry Keller is. I do not want to ruin the movie "Straight Outta Compton" for you but it is Potter to a T. I, as commish, will never let this Jew break up our Crew. Jerry Keller ended up with the most money from NWA, this is going to hurt, Potter has earned the most money from Lewis St. No Pot that is not props, everyone hates you. Potter's name makes me laugh as I am almost as high as his blood pressure. His line up does have the potential to be good but let's be real even Hitler's reign came to and end.

Dr. Dre - Trey - Yo man, it's alot of brothers out there flakin and perpetratin but scared to kick reality so I've been expressin myself. I am still the the T.R.E. Yall motherfuckers will never forget. I am the doctor you need. Always doing all this dope producing, I never get the love I deserve. Even when I am on vacation yall can not forget about T.R.E. When Roger Goodell retires after this year, make me the commish of the NFL. Yall already know.

Suge Knight - Rex - Without question the biggest bully in the rap game. Rex was the biggest bully on Lewis St. He beat up Taco one time to get me on his side. Rex for the amount of times I have fried you on deez nutz jokes I can't believe you would try to make that your team name. I'm disappointed. Your team looks about as strong as Peyton's arm. Not good. Four years ago, you would have the best team ever but it is 2015 bro! Johnson and Johnson hurts my eyes worse than the shampoo. Maybe you will pull it together but your former "oracle(Alec Scercy)" let it out of the bag the other day that he led you with fantasy advice for years. Wow.

Ol Dirty Bastard - Clod - Do I need to explain this one?
