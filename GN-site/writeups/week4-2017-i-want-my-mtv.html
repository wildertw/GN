Week 4 Write Up: I want my MTV

A third of the way through the year and I don't hate nearly as many of you as I much as I would like to. This weeks category will be MTV shows. Much like MTV used to show music videos, I used to do write ups every week. How the mighty have fallen. The league has began to separate like oil and water but with eight weeks to go, anything can happen(but probably not).

1. Trey - MTV Unplugged - Nirvana, Eric Clapton, Jay-z & The Roots, Trey Wilder. Luckily for yall, all of my write ups are as great as unplugged. Electrifying audiences all over the globe. Four weeks, Four wins, speakers for itself. Hopefully the loss of Dalvin Cook doesn't turn me into Unplugged 2.0.

2. Potter - My Super Sweet 16 - Potters going to claim he ever saw this show or knows what it is. Lies. Similar to the show, Potter is spoiled to even have a good team yet finds a way to throw tantrums about them. I am sure theres at least one of those kids who starts smoking crack and falls apart. That is what I expect Potter and his team to do.

3. Ian - Cribs - Step inside Ians crib to see his three headed statue of Brady, Bell, and Elliot staring at you. Jealousy immediately takes over, how could one man own such a trio. Then you go to the bathroom expecting to sit on gold porcelain throne, only to be greeted by a Shell bathroon(one of those with the squishy seats) with a huge dump that is the rest of Ians team. Ians trio will be hard to top if he can flush the shit.

4. Waivers - Jersey Shore - GymTanLaundry, Waivers basically lives by these words these days. I promise I am not going to keep repeating the same punch lines but this one was way too easy. Mikey W's blowout does not have shit on Pauly's. Pussy everywhere. Fist Pumpin everywhere. The only league member with Jersey roots. His teams not half bad either.

5. Rex - Laguna Beach - For the first time in league history we had a trade between Galt and Rex that had no drama following. I am applaud. The drama that normally comes with this is equal to the show. The show was unsurprisingly poorly scripted, like Rex's pick up lines. Rex had possibly the luckiest win in league history like this show getting a third season. Now starting Lee and Parker, maybe trading Cooks away wasn't the wisest decision.

6. Clod - True Life: I am Clod - I got nothing, that speaks for itself.

7. Hud - Jackass - Again, speaks for itself.

8. Shain - Pimp My Ride - Shain's team is in clear need of clean up. I don't like giving out fantasy tips to anyone but I will this once. Do not ever under any circumstance start a defense against the Patriots as long as Tom Brady is around. It is that simple. The outcome would have likely been the same but you never know. I am not sure if even X to Z could Pimp Shain's team..

9. Wood - Punk'd - Taco got punk'd by having Chris Thompson fumble with no time left to lose the match up. It was even the kid's birthday and that karma kicked him square in the balls. This is the kind of luck that lead me to getting the Marlowe last year and it seems to happening to Taco now.

10. Galt - Real World - Galt's team changes as often as the Real World cast(it's every year). With the most acquisitions and changes in the league by a mile. I don't think Galt has the worst team in the league but I can't tell. Yet this is what happens in this league when people stop being nice and start being real. Only one way to go from here.
