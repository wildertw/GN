I reserve the right to go back and use this category again if it ever tickles my fancy again. We’re entering the home stretch boys. This is not fun to write when I have been getting shat on weekly but being the outstanding commissioner that I am…I will. I wasn’t planning on crying this evening but I decided since Clod currently holds the top spot, we’ll do 80s country songs. No George Strait songs were included in this list. Before Potter’s blood pressure rises, I have done the King in more than one comparison already and he could(and might) have his own list. Reminder these are not my top 10 songs. Without further ado…

  1. Clod - “He stopped loving her today” - Clod shocked the league when he decided to not keep Puka, Kyren, Bucky, or Bowers. That decision thwarted Clod into a 6-1 record, and what does the dirty dog do? He blows his team up. Trading away two players who were at the time in the top 10 if not top 5 at their position. His team hasn’t shown any bit of cooling off or slowing down. It is hard to argue today that his team doesn’t look BETTER than they did two weeks ago. Touche Clod
  2. Hud - “Love in the First Degree” - Hud called me last week and told me that this is the first time in sixteen years he has had fun playing fantasy football. He has found a love many of us found in our 20s. Hud is now a staggering 13-2 over his last fifteen games. That’s with a loss last week. Our regular season is only thirteen games. He did not make the playoffs last year. Is anything but a championship now a bad season for Hud? How the times change…
  3. Rex - “Forever and Ever, Amen” - Every night before bed little ole Rex says a prayer. I can’t go into too many details but I know how it ends, “... thanks for letting me be able to keep Bijan Robinson forever and ever, amen”. Despite that, Rex’s team seems to go as Drake London goes. Over the last six weeks he is (3-3), in his three wins Drake was the WR1 overall on the week. In two of his three losses Drake was either hurt or on bye. The good news for Rex is he has the easiest remaining schedule in the league statistically.
  4. Ian - “9 to 5” - Fantasy is work for Ian’s team. They get in there and get the job done. Week in and week out. Year after year. Ian has a (54-27) weekly power record. Which is good for second best on the season. Yet, he sits right outside of the playoff bubble with a (4-5) record. He plays two critical matchups with Potter and Rex over the next two weeks, who sit ahead of team Baro by one win.
  5. Waivers - “Copperhead Road” - Fun fact not many people know about Waivers. He doesn’t just like Copperhead Road, he fucking LOVES Copperhead Road. Every Friday and sometimes Saturday night at 1:00 A.M. you could find him slappin his boot and gettin down to Copperhead Road on the Buc dance floor. He was a menace. Made a 72 year woman go to the hospital one time after she swooned after he tipped his cap to her. After 27 career matchups, Waivers and I now have a point differential of 0.9. (Mindblown Emoji). The matchup this week against myself will be crucial for Waiver’s playoff hopes.
  6. Potter - “I’m no Stranger to the Rain” - I am willing to bet the guys around Potter’s office think he gets screwed over right and left in fantasy football. You know he brags about his five championships to them. I heard he has even brought the trophy to the office. After a week 2 loss to Clod by 0.9, Potter has a 2 points by win vs me, a 1.9 point victory over Waivers, and a 0.8 victory of Wood. Those three matchups could have swung either way. Some will say the better man won. He now faces most likely the hardest remaining schedule with matchups against Ian, Clod, Hud, and myself.
  7. Shain - “Swingin” - Shain was swingin for fences trading away two key players in Michael Pittman and ETN for Josh Jacobs. I got to say I have liked this trade for both sides more and more. Shain faces the top of the league over the next two weeks in Clod and Hud. If he can pull off wins there, his last two weeks are much easier on paper with matchups again Lee and myself
  8. Trey - “Two Dozen Roses” - Two dozen roses and an old bottle of wine are about the only hope I have left in this season.
  9. Lee - “Fishin in the Dark” - With all the injuries to Galt’s team he has to feel like hes fishin in dark. It hasn’t been all doom and gloom for Galt’s team, he picked up a win over Ian last week in a 74-70 barn burner. Lee smartly benched his D leaving points on the table but you do what you have to do to get the win. Lee and I are going to be fighting one another for who will play Wood in the caddy bowl I fear.
  10. Wood - “Bop” - There’s no nice way to put this. Wood has been getting bopped weekly. The last two weeks have to taken whatever wind was left in the Wood sails has been deflated. A 0.8 loss to Potter that easily could have been won with 9 more Kareem Hunt rushing yards. And a comeback win by Clod on Sunday Night Football on the back(arm) of Sam Darnold.
