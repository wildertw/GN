Lewis meets Sesame St

Write up 2015: Lewis meets Sesame St

First things first, there should be a advertisement on the right side of the screen that says "Papa John's commissioner of the week" all of you need to click on it several times. Next we need to congratulate young Shain on his record breaking week. Two claps and a Rick Flair should do it.

The write up this week will compare everyones team to a Sesame Street character. Now where Sesame Street is sunshine, rainbows, and butterflies; Lewis St could not be further from this. Liquor and whores all day.

Elmo - Galt - Galt has to be tickled with how his team has panned out thus far. If Brady stays upright, Galt almost seems like a lock to make the playoffs. He'll be putting his hands in the air screaming, ayooo I ain't Elmoooo. Antonio Brown catching passes from Vick should not hurt his value. Does anyone think Galt looks like Elmo? I do. The tickle me version of Galt must have malfunctioned, they normally do two test tickles before going on shelves. Galt got none.

Oscar - Potter - At an early age Pot's mom could not figure out why his favorite character was Oscar. Now at age 27, it all makes sense. Judging by his normal temperament you would think he lives in a fucking trash can. Pouting about how he has the worst _______(insert some variation of bullshit). His team name is not true. Never Calm. One man's trash is another man's treasure, and Potter found some treasures in the draft. Will his team prevail or will Potter be kicking it with Oscar in the trash?

Cookie Monster - Rex - One in the same, both are known for their voracious appetite and famous eating phrases: "Me want tilapia/cookie". This past week he wanted a big game from mal and mal delivered giving Lee his first loss of the season. Cookie Monster believes his name used to Sid, Rex believes his name used to be Taylor. Rex has to have the oldest average team in the league but right now they look solid. Can their old bag of bones hold up for a whole season or will they crumble like a stale cookie.

Grover - Hud - Not to beat a dead horse but Grover does not have any ears and lets be real, Shaq's hands are smaller than Huds. They do both have those pea sized heads. I am proud of you for finally changing your team name from that shit name you had but this one has already grown old and tiresome.

Big Bird - Waivers - Now before Waivers says it, I am sure there will some kind of semi-witty generic response to this saying "No way Trey you're Big Bird. You're the tall and goofy one. Hahut hahut.". Still waiting for a stat correction to fuck me over but I have never been so excited over an extra point that meant not a damn thing in a real game. It would have been some big bullshit if we would have tied. Do not try to act like you don't care, I know, I know. Waivers thought his bird was going to crow but it looks as though he needs to make a trade.

Kermit the Frog - Shain - Hiyoooo Shailine the Frog here. Shain could be the puppet master for Kermit. I happen to have heard his impersonation of Kermit, spot on. Shain had a week for the ages last week. Who saw that coming? No one. Sitting Eddy Lacy and ending up with the weekly scoring title. Wowzers. Shains team hit on all cylinders last week but I still can not take him out of the running for having Ms. Piggy in his house at the end of the year.

The Count - Ian - E and The Count both haves the most distinguishable laughs on both of their respective streets. E counted ONE, TWO, two quarterbacks taken in the first eight rounds. Ha HAHAHA. Now with Drew Brees ailing a shoulder injury it will be hard to pawn him off like he originally planned. E draws me this week and things are already not looking good as several of his players are dealing with injuries and are unlikely to play. He could pull a Shain but let's be real.

Bert - Trey - I am going to be a Bert with no Ernie as I can not make my last two my side kick. I may or may not physically resemble Bert. Generally a nice and intelligent, we are both also grump, boring, and easily irritated. No one wants to hear my gloat about myself. I get to look in the mirror every day to see this perfect human being, not yall.

Snuffaluffagus - Taco -Snuffaluffagus is actually just big mammoth creature that Big Bird(Waivers) sees when he is tripping acid. You read that right, Taco is actually just a figment of everyone's imagination. Without Dez, Wood went through a week or so where all I could hear was him snuffling in his room. His team is mediocre at best, it is my own hope that they can be just that to keep Maddy out of his house. Though he may go full deja vu, and place his balls over Maddy's eyes and dick over her nose aka The Snuffaluffagus.

Slimy the Worm - Clod - Slimy is the pet of Oscar for those who have not been up on their sesame street making this a comparison spot on. Clod's team is Slimey. It is not easy on the eyes for me, I can only imagine how he feels about it. Every time I can look at it I go "EWWWWWW". Being compared to the orange Slimey shoud be the worst of Clod's orange fears as he almost seems destined to have the Ginger Dragon lurking in his house.
