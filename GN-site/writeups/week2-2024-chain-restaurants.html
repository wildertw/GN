2024 · Week 2

Chain Restaurants

Welcome to Year 16, where 16+ years of friendship and rivalry continue to fuel the heart of this fantasy football league. Fantasy football is the only game where you can spend months building excitement and anticipation, only to have your dreams crushed by Week 3, leaving you ready to throw in the towel. Yet, somehow, we always come back for more.

We’ve come a long way since the days of the “Big Dawg,” “Stinky Muff,” and the iconic “Al Davis: Manager Play of the Week” awards. Now, we boast our own league website, complete with weekly power rankings. Heck, even T. Wood shows up in the group chat these days (just kidding, but maybe this year is different?).

As we kick off this season, what better way to get things started than by comparing everyone’s teams to chain restaurants? And yes, Hud is already fired up about it. These are all national sit-down chains—no Moes, Five Guys, or McAlister’s. Let the roast begin!

Here’s to another season of heartbreak, trash talk, and maybe—just maybe—a championship for someone other than the usual suspects. Let's go!

Rex’s Roadhouse - Taylor Hardee: Rex's fantasy team has him salivating like he's just been served a perfectly seared steak. That trade with Galt? It was like spotting a half-eaten NY strip on someone else's plate after already devouring your own. Greedy, but effective.

Wilder Garden - Trey Wilder: The team might be exposed as frauds after losing Pacheco. Let's be honest, it's all breadsticks and soup at this point—filling, but not enough to keep you satisfied.

Baro Wild Wings - Ian Baro: EW3’s was almost too obvious here. Wings. Beer. Fantasy Football. Rumor has it, Ian gathers the local coaching circle for lunch beers and pep talks on Mondays and Wednesdays at BW3s. Last week, the Harbaugh and Manning brothers were allegedly in attendance. Legends only, right?

Waiver’s & Busters - Michael Waivers: In classic Waivers fashion, he lost his wallet (CMC) the second he walked in the door but found a leftover credit (Jordan Mason) at Pop the Lock and hit the jackpot. He’s hoping to turn those tickets into a trophy—or just swipe one from Galt.

Po(u)tback - Mark Potter: Doom and gloom over at Potter’s camp. The man’s been the second-highest scorer only to play the top scorer two weeks in a row. But hey, have you tried the bloomin' onion? It might cheer you up.

Cody Tuesdays - Cody Clodfelter: Like the salad bar at Ruby Tuesday’s, Clod's team might be getting overlooked. Sure, it doesn't scream excitement, but it could surprise you when you're not paying attention.

Collin’s Corral - Shain Collins: Shain's making his third trip to the QB buffet in as many weeks. Half of his team now has the same designation as Golden Corral’s food, Questionable.

TWD Fridays - T Wood: If you've never been to a TGI Fridays with T Wood in his prime, you've truly missed out. The apps were endless, the stories were legendary. As for his fantasy team, the 27th annual Taylor Bowl kicks off Thursday, currently tied at 13-13. Buckle up.

AppleLees Lee Galt: Drafting five straight WRs is about as wild as hitting up Applebee's on a Tuesday lunch for $1 Long Islands. And now, Galt's trading away his 4th-round pick, Drake London, for Rex’s 10th-round pick, Zack Moss. Do we even need the Applebee’s 3 for $10 trade analyzer to call this one?

Huddars - Josh Hudson: Fun fact: Hud's managed to score exactly 88.1 points in two consecutive weeks. Has it happened before? Who cares. His team’s performance is about as thrilling as Cheddar's food (which, by the way, I've never had the displeasure of trying)—bland and unsatisfying. But he's probably going to bounce back this week against me.
