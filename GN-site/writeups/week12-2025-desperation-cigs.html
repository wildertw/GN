2025 · Week 12

Desperation Cigs

Two weeks to go in the regular season and I won last week so let's do a write up. The injury bug unfortunately caught a few top 20 stars this past and now the final sprint for the final two playoff spots can go any which way. According to Chat GPT there are 1024 possible scenarios that can play out over the last two weeks. I asked Waivers if that was true but he hasn’t texted me back. The end of the season has me stressed so let's do cigarette brands.

Cody Clodfelter (10–1, 1288.6 PF) - Marlboro

The undisputed (current) king of the league, Marlboro energy. Everyone knows it, everyone respects it(not really), and everyone’s tired of seeing it but there’s no denying the results. Clods always had a cowboy fantasy, it’s just probably not the first one you thought of. He clinched playoffs weeks ago, smoking the competition. Somehow the only path for anyone to have a 10-1 record would be Cody play codys schedule. Is that luck or just a guy who is always in the right place at the right time?

  • Can only finish 10–3, 11–2, or 12–1
  • Playoff Odds: 100%
  • Already clinched, no outcome exists where he falls outside Top 4.

Josh Hudson (9–2, 1266.5 PF) - Camel

The camel in Jamaar Chase could cost Hud a victory this week. That’s a spitting joke for those that didn’t get it. Old school, smooth, dependable, never flashy, always in control...most of these things do not describe Hud but do describe the best cigarette in the world. Doesn’t need hype, just wins. Camel confidence. Even a 2-week collapse wouldn’t shake him out of Top 4.

Playoff Odds: 99%

  • Mathematically safe, even if he drops both and finishes 9–4, it’s still a Top 4 record in almost all plausible outcomes

Rex – 6–5, 1284.7 PF - Parliament

The bougie cigarette for when Waivers was feeling classy. High PF, strong résumé, and expects to be a playoff team. If Rex goes 2-0, he’s in. If he slips once, things get complicated fast. The one thing Rex could not take was a loss to one of his two star wide receivers. Of course the voodoo king himself, Waivers, called upon his good friend Kendrick Lamar to put a curse on Rex’s Drake this week with a double whammy knee injury and Penix going down.

Playoff odds: ~75%

Remaining: vs Waivers, vs T Wood

  • If Rex wins out (2–0 → 8–5):
     🔒 Guaranteed in.
  • If Rex goes 1–1 (7–6):
     Still in good shape because of very strong Points For. Needs at least one of Waivers / Potter / Ian to stumble and not finish clearly ahead.
  • If Rex goes 0–2 (6–7):
     He can still be caught by Trey/Shain if they win out and also pass him in Points For, plus other contenders also fall to 6–7.

Waivers (was Michael) – 6–5, 1233.1 PF - Newports

Newports, confidence, bold, loud, dramatic, and always in the mix. 2-0? He’s in. 1-1? Stress levels are high. 0-2? Things get really interesting. Waivers has had many a night end with a Newport. Some good. Some bad. Which will this be? He is off to a great start with zero waiver wire claims this week even though his QB, WR2, Flex, TE, and D/ST are all on BYE this week. Even allowing Rex to pick up the best QB available.(Disclaimer: I wanted to make Potter Newports for the obvious reason but I knew you guys would call me racist).

Remaining: vs Rex, vs Ian

Playoff odds: ~60%

  • If Waivers wins out (2–0 → 8–5):
     🔒 Guaranteed in.
  • If Waivers goes 1–1 (7–6):
     In the mix. He’s competitive on PF but not as safe as Rex. Needs:
    • At least one of Rex / Potter / Ian not to land at a cleaner record or beat him on PF.
  • If Waivers goes 0–2 (6–7):
     He becomes part of the big 6–7 mess and can be jumped by:
    • Potter (if he does better),
    • Ian (if he finishes better),
    • Trey or Shain if they win out and pass him in Points For.

Mark Potter - 6–5, 1167.8 PF - Lucky Strike

Lucky Strike Mark needs one more big break to get in one huge win or one collapse from others. Can he find another way to eek out another close victory? Can absolutely make the postseason… or absolutely flame out but would get a better draft position.

Playoff odds: ~40%

Remaining: vs Josh, vs Trey

  • If Potter wins out (2–0 → 8–5):
     🔒 Guaranteed in.
  • If Potter goes 1–1 (7–6):
     Still has a shot, but his low Points For makes tiebreaks dangerous. Needs:
    • Not to get stuck in a big 7–6 or 6–7 cluster where Rex/Waivers/Ian all have better PF.
  • If Potter goes 0–2 (6–7):
     Wide open for others to pass him — including Trey or Shain if they win out and beat him in PF.

Ian Baro – 5–6, 1240.4 PF - American Spirits

Organic, slow burn, takes forever to decide if it’s good or not… but when it hits, it hits. If theres one thing Ian’s team is going to bring, it’s spirit. Needs to upset Cody or Waivers to stay alive. Not dead but living on the fringe.

Playoff odds: ~25%

Remaining: vs Cody, vs Waivers

  • If Ian wins out (2–0 → 7–6):
     Very live for a spot, especially if:
    • One of Rex / Waivers / Potter does not get to 8 wins, AND
    • He holds his PF edge over at least one of them.
  • If Ian goes 1–1 (6–7):
     Thin path only: would need a big cluster of 6–7 teams with him near the top in Points For.
  • If Ian goes 0–2 (5–8):
     ❌ Done. And this is exactly the kind of “loses out” scenario that can open a door for Trey/Shain.

Trey Wilder – 4–7, 1179.4 PF - Winston

Blue-collar, gritty, heavy pulls and somehow still alive.
Needs to win out and jump a collapsing team in Points For.
Big weeks and chaos are the only path. Caddy bowl is still way in play.

Playoff odds: ~2%

Remaining: vs Shain, vs Potter

For Trey to make the playoffs, all of this has to happen:

  1. Trey wins out → finishes 6–7.
    • Beats Shain in Week 12
    • Beats Potter in Week 13
  2. Any three of the following four lose out:
    • Rex (6–5 → 6–7)
    • Waivers (6–5 → 6–7)
    • Potter (6–5 → 6–7, and Trey hands him one of those losses)
    • Ian (5–6 → 5–8 or 5–7 range, depending on how you’re counting)
  3. Trey passes enough of them in Points For among the 6–7 group:
    • With two big weeks, he needs to outscore at least one or two of Rex/Waivers/Potter/Ian in PF in that final 6–7 cluster while Cody/Josh sit up top.

If that happens, the final Top 4 could look like:

  • Cody
  • Josh
  • One of Rex/Waivers/Potter/Ian who didn’t collapse
  • Trey sneaking in at 6–7 via PF tiebreak over the other 6–7 teams.

Shain Collins – 4–7, 1067.7 PF - Pall Mall

Absolute wild card. Shain losing Josh Jacobs is as fragrant as a Pall Mall Orange. If you’ve never had the pleasure of smelling a Pall Mall Orange, I will take you to an establishment next time you are in town. Shain needs wins, needs explosions in Points For, needs multiple top-half collapses. It’s possible… but it requires the football gods smoking a full carton. The caddy bowl is still in play.

Playoff odds: ~1%

Remaining: vs Trey, vs Lee

Shain’s path is similar to Trey’s, but his Points For is lower, so he needs even bigger weeks.

For Shain to make the playoffs:

  1. Shain wins out → finishes 6–7.
    • Beats Trey in Week 12
    • Beats Lee in Week 13
  2. Any three of Rex / Waivers / Potter / Ian lose out so that they all end up 6–7 or worse.
  3. Shain absolutely explodes in Points For over the last two weeks:
    • Needs to catch and pass at least one or two of those collapsing teams on PF in that 6–7 logjam..

Lee Galt - 4–7, 1023.1 PF- Kool

Remaining: vs T Wood, vs Shain

  • Even if Lee wins out to 6–7, his very low PF makes it almost impossible to win a multi-team tiebreak vs:
    • Rex
    • Waivers
    • Potter
    • Ian
    • Trey (if Trey also spikes PF)
      .

T Wood – 1–10, 1022.1 PF - Dorals

  • Best possible finish: 3–10

❌ Eliminated.

TLDR;

  • Cody & Josh – Locked into the playoffs.
  • Rex, Waivers, Potter, Ian – Main contenders for the last two spots; control most of the realistic paths.
  • Trey & Shain –
    • Must win out to 6–7, and
    • Need any three of Rex, Waivers, Potter, Ian to lose out, and
    • Must outscore those collapsing teams in Points For to win tiebreaks.
      → Very slim but not zero.
  • Lee & T Wood – Out.
