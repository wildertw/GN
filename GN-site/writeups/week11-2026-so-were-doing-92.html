9–2, So We’reDoing ’92

Greenville Natives, 2026 season. Week 11 recap and Week 12 preview.

Roll the tape

I beat Rex, Clod beat Wood, and I woke up Monday 9–2 and back at number one. So this week we’re doing songs from 1992. Is that a little self-serving? Boys, I built an entire website so you could read what I think about ten fantasy football teams, one of which is mine. I thought we settled that question years ago.

Roll the tape from Kansas City. Mahomes drops back, finds Kelce, touchdown. Wood jumps up. Clod jumps up. Wood remembers whose tight end that is and sits his ass back down. Now over to Dallas, where Dak finds CeeDee and Waivers gets about half a second to enjoy himself before Hud reminds him how passing touchdowns work. Two games, four managers, and the only guy who got to celebrate a touchdown in peace was Galt, who got three from Jonah Coleman and dedicated every damn one of them to Potter.

Two regular-season games left. Rex and I are in, Wood needs one more win, and Hud holds the spot three guys at 5–6 want. Meanwhile, Shain beat Ian and earned the right to explain six separate football games to his family over Thanksgiving dinner. Before we get to the mixtape, Hermania would like a word with Ian’s defensive coordinator.
