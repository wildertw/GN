2023 · Week 9

2000s Movies

We are entering week 10 with Hud and Shain having a clear lead of the rest of the field. What. The. ****. Is. Going. On. Their strengths massively outweigh their weaknesses. Poor luck and/or coaching will be the only thing keeping them from the play offs. Their week 12 matchup will likely be for the 1 seed. We have never had an 8 win team miss the playoffs(granted there is an extra regular season game but 8 has been the “magic number” since we went to 13 games). 3 of the last 4 champions have been the 1 seed. Since moving to super division Wood is the only champion to not be the 1 or 2 seed(he was 3rd). Week 9 was a wild one. We had upsets in almost every matchup. Capped off by a JHud Monday night victory by a mere 1.9 points. I was hoping to get my team's playoff hopes back on track with a win against Potter. Even had the write up theme picked, “Sex positions”, Potter was going to get anal(still can). Week 10 will be do or die for many teams. I have changed topics three times now. Started with football owners but I can only name 7 maybe 8 owners. Will try it again as a post draft write up. Moved to fast food restaurants. 100% was going to get Rex with a “Wendy’s nutz go in your mouth”.. Landed on 2000s movies(edit: half way through writing this it hit me I did a movie theme last week and significantly lost interest). I’ve done 90s movies before. Did not include any of the LotR, Marvel, Pixar, Disney, Batman, or any other movies I have used before. Lastly, this is NOT a mount rushmore ranking.

Shain Collins - Catch me if you can - Shain has continued his tear. Unfortunately for Shainy, he's no longer in sole lead after the Monday Night Hud Miracle. He would still be in first place if not for his decision to start the New York Football Giants D over the Chiefs D. Regardless,

Josh Hudson - Man on Fire - [insert NBA Jams “You’re on FIRE!”] Currently riding a 7 game winning streak. Season high and his personal best. Can they keep it up against team Wilder this week? Does Hud’s team have a weakness? D? Tight end? After the years of abuse Huds team has taken I am sure they are ok with bringing subpar D with a not so tight end.

Lee Galt - V for Vendetta - Remember, remember the 2nd of January. A masked griddying avenger, across the land, Like V for Vendetta, he fights the night, With fiery eyes and will of might. Galt is clearly out for blood after his championship* loss last season.

Ian Baro - 300 - Coach E has been known to show “300” on their away game flights. Jay Glazer reports “several players were seen vomiting on the sidelines after Monday’s practice.” Glazer also noted you could smell the disappointment coming from the coach. Not only is Ian’s team angry they face Potter this week who they currently have a seven game streak. If I can ever figure out AI art or decide to buy photoshop I would 100% put Ian’s face on Leonidis kicking Potter into the hole.

Taylor Hardee - The Hurt Locker - This has nothing to do with the actual movie and everything to do with the title. It’s not even about how Rex has had to overcome injuries. It’s about how butt hurt he is that Arthur Smith refuses to play with his Bajina.

When asked in his press conference why Potter loves Adams so much he replied to reporters “"He is the sweetest guy. Have you ever looked into his eyes? It was like the first time I heard the Beatles."
