2020 · Week 1

Cereal

Sweet beginnings

Year 12. Love. Hate. Deception. We somehow made it into our 12th year. I'll make my annual pre season promise of doing these more often but lets be real. I was going to start off comparing everyone to the most diabolical world leaders in history but calling Potter Hitler is only funny so many times. Then I was and still might to STDs just didnt want to offend any of you. I'll attempt to open the season a sweeter note to get all the salt by (slightly biased) ranking everyones teams based on cereals.

1) Lucky Charms - The Goatfather - Like it was going to be anybody else

2) Cinnamon Toast Crunch - Make Hud Great Again - The best part about CTC is the milk at the end. If Huds team can avoid going stale, they have a solid chance to not only break the 5 year playoffs drought but win Hud's first title. I'm not looking but I'm pretty sure Hud has the worst win percentage in the league so that seems likely to hold him back.

3) Honey Bunches of Oats - Mikedale Stoopboys - I wanted to pick on Waivers team for something but I couldn't just like many of you want to hate on Honey Bunches of Oats but cant. Solid all around, hard to find a flaw. Fun Fact: Waivers team will seek not only his first championship but to be the first relocation team to win a ship. Although karma from the lifetime of Waivers of scheming has normally kept him away.

4) Count Chocula - Squirrel trying To get a Nut - Even though it was discontinued last year. the count still makes the list. I actually believe this is the second time Ian's made the list as the count. The first was his sesame street counter part. Unfortunately, I see Ians team aging like cereal, warm and soggy.

5) Cheerios - Keep Conn and Carry on - Plain jane. Its missing something. Honey nut cheerios, bomb. Banana Nut Cheerios even better. Shains team lacks a wow factor. While having a strong receiving core, he again will have a revolving door at running back behind DHenry. They say Week 1 everyone has a chance. Go Pirates!

6) Fruit Loops - Hold My CHUBB - Never forgetten and in the top tier of cereals, Rexboy has put together another solid team after being gifted CMaC a year after winning it all. Rex was in shock the first time I brought home a box of Fruit loops and a half gallon of milk. He told me he never had Fruit Loops growing up. I refused to share with him, after wards he stole goats bowl, grabbed his Fruity-Os and cut open his bag of milk. Grunted the night away in the corner.

7) Raisin Bran - Ball so Hard University - The self proclaimed cereal connoisseur of the league will understand his comparison. Will tacos reign of bleacher report updates continue to bring success or will falter. Personally I feel as Raisin Bran is constantly slept on.

8) Frosted Flakes - Auto Draft - Yes, yes this is a hair joke about Potter.

9) Trix - Boe Jiden - Galt continued his tricks into this year, reaching for Travis Kelce with 11th overall selection. Having your starting QB and RB is never a good thing in fantasy. Rostering only 3 WRs. Hes always got a trick up his sleeve so maybe he will prove me wrong but...

10) Fruity Pebbles - Clod
