2014 · Week 0

Draft Recap College Edition

Congrats everyone we made it through another draft without Rex crying. Thats 1.5 drafts a new league record! This year for the post draft write up I have decided to compare everyones team to college football teams. Now some/most of you are not going to agree with your team. I did not base the teams off of rankings as much as I did off of the actual team. If you don't know much about college football....sucks.

Florida St. - Trey - Who else did yall think this was going to be? I am number one in my power rankings until I am proven otherwise. With a Heisman QB in Jameis and a potential MVP in Luck, I feel very strong about both of my quarterbacks. Running back is a bit of question mark, I got autodrafted for the first time in draft history with Marshawn. Ellington and/or Gerhart need to pan out for my team to make a playoff push. Having Dez fall to me in round 2 could not have gone better, Keenan Allen should continue to thrive in San Diego. Enough about my team, I obviously like them.

Alabama - Rex - A former champion and a strong running game has Rex screamin Roll Tide. The three headed monster of Jamaal Alfred and Rashad Jenning should work well this season. Amoni Cooper along with Jordy Nelson are both two of the top receivers in there respected levels of play. There are some things I do question about Rex, other than his selection in shirt size, his receivers outside of Jordy are mainly boom or bust receivers this year. Can Matt Ryan return to top tier status? We shall find out soon

Oregon - Shain - Shain and Oregon seem to have the same problems, they always seem to have a great year but never make it all the way and injuries are normally the cause. Shain has maybe the best QB in fantasy in ARod and Oregon could have the best in Marcus Marriota. Oregons run game is always top tier and with Eddy Lacy leading the way for Shain, his rushing attack should be tops. A receiving core base with low end WR1s-high end WR2s in Jeffery Garcon and Crabtree should have Shain looking good to start the year once again.

Auburn - Hud - Auburns rushing attack compares to few on the college football scene and Huds team has the potential to have a great one lead by Forte and Bell(how is he not suspended?). And although Drew Brees and Nick Marshall are nothing alike they are both crucial to their teams and put up big points week in and week out. Huds question marks come on the receiving side like Auburn. They are laced with boom or bust picks, which I am sure will help him some weeks but will kill him in others. Hud lives a life of boom or bust picks so it could work out for him.

Georgia - Waivers - Georgias Todd Gurley is hands down the best running back in the land and AP has lived on that island for many years. AJ Green went to Georgia. And with second running backs Zac Stacy and Ryan Matthews, Waivers and Georgia could have some of the best RB2s around. Question marks around waivers team starts with Nick Foles, can he possibly repeat what he did last year? The NFL has had a whole offseason to study tape on him. Can Julian Edleman continue to be Tom Bradys top dog?

Ohio St - Potter - Ohio St lost Heisman hopeful Braxton Miller and is now set to see if backup QB Barret now set to lead the charge with several team tier weapons but also has question marks. Potters team has the chance to be unstoppable, the best comparison I can think of is betting on red everytime in roulette. It can work but boy o boy can it fail. Brady Gronk Foster Martin Foster Murray A Brown could all be in the top 5 of their positions this year. They also all could finish outside of the top 20. Potter is as Jewish as you can get when it comes to gambling and this draft has me thinkin Caroline finally made him get circumcised.

Baylor - Ian - I hope everyone let Ian know that as a rookie once every two weeks he is obligated to buy the rest of the league a shot. Ians team along with Baylor have one of the most feared passing attacks around. Baylor led by QB Bryce Petty is a modern day gunslinger along with E's QB Matt Stafford. Calvin and Demariyus could be the two best wide receivers in fantasy land. Ians question marks come with his run game. Gore Spiller and CJ2k have all seen their shine time in the past but will this be the end for Gore? Can Spiller stay healthy? Can Chris Johnson speak English?

LSU - Lee - LSU's coach Les Miles nickname is The Mad Hatter and if had to pick one person in the league to call the mad hatter it would without a doubt be Galt. Lee will have question marks every week with who to start between Cam and RG3. Montee Ball and Jimmy Graham could finish atop their position with Cobb and Martin potentially finish in the the top 10. Lee's team looks strong but as we all know Lee is always active in the trade market. Could this be the year Lee's annual blockbuster trade works out in his favor.

Stanford - Taco - For anyone who cares season 5 of the league just came out on netflix. We could have a diamond in the rough with our taco. Taco spent much of the draft staring at me instead of actually paying attention to his own team. Tacos core of Peyton B Marsh and J Thomas is as solid as they come. Running back could hurt Taco but the more I look at it the more I like it, Bush S Jax and Trent could all end up being high end RB2s. Wes welker might have taken the molly but taco should be the one sweatin.
